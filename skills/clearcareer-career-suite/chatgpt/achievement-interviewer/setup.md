# Setup: ClearCareer AI Achievement Interviewer (ChatGPT Custom GPT)

1. In ChatGPT, open **Explore GPTs > Create** (or go to chatgpt.com/gpts/editor) and choose the **Configure** tab.
2. **Name:** ClearCareer AI Achievement Interviewer
3. **Description:** Upload your resume, then talk it through in voice mode. It asks one question at a time until every role has numbers on it, then writes your bullets and 8 professional summaries.
4. **Instructions:** paste everything in `instructions.md`.
5. **Knowledge:** upload every file in the `knowledge/` folder (career-suite-guide.md, master-career-profile-template.md, quantification-playbook.md, question-bank.md, voice-mode-guide.md, writing-rules.md).
6. **Conversation starters:**
   - Here's my resume. Start my achievement interview.
   - Start my achievement interview. Use my Voice Interview Brief.
   - Rebuild my profile
   - Write my bullets and professional summaries
7. **Capabilities:** turn on Canvas. Code Interpreter is optional (helps read PDF resumes and check math).
8. Test it with a sample resume, then share the GPT link with members.

Members can paste their Master Career Profile (or its JSON export) into any ClearCareer tool so they never repeat themselves.
