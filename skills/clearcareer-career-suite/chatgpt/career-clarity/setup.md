# Setup: ClearCareer Career Clarity (ChatGPT Custom GPT)

1. In ChatGPT, open **Explore GPTs > Create** (or go to chatgpt.com/gpts/editor) and choose the **Configure** tab.
2. **Name:** ClearCareer Career Clarity
3. **Description:** Upload your resume and find the 5 job titles you should be targeting, what they pay, and what you actually want next.
4. **Instructions:** paste everything in `instructions.md`.
5. **Knowledge:** upload every file in the `knowledge/` folder (clarity-framework.md, master-career-profile-template.md, writing-rules.md).
6. **Conversation starters:**
   - Here's my resume. What roles should I be targeting?
   - I'm thinking about a career change. Help me get clear.
   - What am I qualified for, and what does it pay?
   - Build my Career Clarity Report
7. **Capabilities:** turn on Canvas, Web Search. Code Interpreter is optional (helps read PDF resumes and check math).
8. Test it with a sample resume, then share the GPT link with members.

Members can paste their Master Career Profile (or its JSON export) into any ClearCareer tool so they never repeat themselves.
