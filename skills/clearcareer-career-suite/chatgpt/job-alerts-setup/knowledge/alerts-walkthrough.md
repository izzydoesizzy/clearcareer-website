# Job Alerts: Click-by-Click Walkthrough


Menu names change. If something doesn't match, tell them to look for a "Set alert", "Get notifications", "Follow" or bell icon near the search results.

## LinkedIn (one alert per title cluster)
1. Go to linkedin.com/jobs (or the Jobs tab in the app).
2. Paste the string into the search box. Put the city, country, or "Remote" in the location box. Press search.
3. Set the filters: **Date posted:** Past 24 hours. **Experience level:** pick their level and one above. **Remote:** pick what works. Leave **Easy Apply** off so you see every job.
4. Check the first page of results. If more than half look wrong, add one exclusion (NOT intern) or remove a title, and search again.
5. Turn on the **Set alert** toggle at the top of the results (it may say "Job alert" or show a bell).
6. Choose **Daily** and turn on both email and app notifications. On a phone you may find this under the alert settings in the Jobs tab, or in Settings > Notifications > Searching for a job.
7. To check or edit alerts later: Jobs > Preferences > Job alerts (or "Manage job alerts").
8. Repeat for each cluster. Keep it to 3 to 5 alerts total so the emails stay readable.

## Google Jobs
1. Go to google.com and search the Google Jobs query, for example: `"Customer Success Manager" jobs Toronto`.
2. Click into the jobs panel ("Jobs" box or the Jobs tab).
3. Set filters like Date posted (Past day or Past 3 days), Type and Work from home.
4. If you see a **Get notifications**, **Follow** or bell toggle, turn it on. Google will email new matching jobs. This option isn't shown in every country or account.
5. If you don't see it, use the Google Alerts method below with the title-plus-city query, and run the X-ray searches (Tools > Past 24 hours) as a daily habit or a scheduled task.

## Google Alerts (company hiring and news signals)
1. Go to google.com/alerts while signed in to Google.
2. Paste one query from your pack into the box.
3. Click **Show options**: How often: At most once a day. Sources: Automatic (or News + Web). Language and Region: yours. How many: Only the best results. Deliver to: your email.
4. Click **Create Alert**. Repeat for each query.
5. Tip: create a Gmail filter that labels these as "Job Alerts" so they don't bury your inbox.

## Indeed (optional)
1. Go to indeed.com, paste the Indeed string into "What" and your city or "Remote" into "Where".
2. Set Date posted to Last 24 hours.
3. Click **Get new jobs for this search by email** (or the bell / "Turn on job alerts") and confirm your email.
