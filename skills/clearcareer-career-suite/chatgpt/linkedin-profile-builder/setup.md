# Setup: ClearCareer LinkedIn Profile Builder (ChatGPT Custom GPT)

1. In ChatGPT, open **Explore GPTs > Create** (or go to chatgpt.com/gpts/editor) and choose the **Configure** tab.
2. **Name:** ClearCareer LinkedIn Profile Builder
3. **Description:** Rebuilds your LinkedIn headline, About, experience, skills and Open to Work settings from your Master Career Profile, ready to paste.
4. **Instructions:** paste everything in `instructions.md`.
5. **Knowledge:** upload every file in the `knowledge/` folder (career-suite-guide.md, linkedin-playbook.md, master-career-profile-template.md, writing-rules.md).
6. **Conversation starters:**
   - Here's my Master Career Profile. Build my LinkedIn profile.
   - Write 10 LinkedIn headlines for my target title
   - Rewrite my LinkedIn About section
   - Score my current LinkedIn profile
7. **Capabilities:** turn on Canvas. Code Interpreter is optional (helps read PDF resumes and check math).
8. Test it with your resume. The GPT is private to you unless you share it.

Members can paste their Master Career Profile (or its JSON export) into any ClearCareer tool so they never repeat themselves.
