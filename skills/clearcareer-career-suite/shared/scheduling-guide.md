# Scheduling Guide: Recurring Job Searches in Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini

Each scheduled run starts fresh with no memory of this conversation. That's why the job-search prompt is fully self-contained: it carries the client's profile summary, search strings and the scoring rubric inside it.

Menu names on these platforms change often. Give the steps below, and if something doesn't match, tell the client to look for "Scheduled", "Tasks" or "Scheduled actions" in the sidebar or settings, or simply ask the assistant in a normal chat: "Run this every weekday at 8am."

## Recommended schedule

| Task | When | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Daily Job Scout | Weekdays, 7 to 8am local | New postings from the last 24 to 72 hours, scored, top 3 to apply to today |
| Weekly Target Company Sweep | Monday morning | Checks every target company's careers page for matching roles |
| Weekly Market Expansion Scout | Thursday morning | Looks for title families the client isn't searching yet that fit their proven experience |
| Weekly Scorecard Check-in | Friday afternoon | 10 quick questions on how the search is going, with one fix for next week |

Most platforms cap active scheduled tasks (often around 10). Four is plenty.

## Claude

- **Best:** if Claude can create scheduled tasks in this conversation (a scheduling tool is available), offer to create them directly. Confirm the time zone and times first.
- **Manual:** in the Claude app or claude.ai, open **Scheduled** in the left sidebar, click **New task**, paste the task prompt, set the schedule (for example, weekdays at 7:45am) and save. Scheduled tasks need a paid plan.
- Make sure web search is turned on for the task. If the client has Gmail connected, the task can also read LinkedIn and Indeed alert emails.

## ChatGPT

- Scheduled tasks don't run inside custom GPTs or voice chats. Tell the client to open a **new normal chat** (not the GPT), paste the task prompt, and add the schedule line at the top, for example: "Run this every weekday at 7:45am Eastern."
- ChatGPT confirms and creates the task. Manage it from **Scheduled** (or "See scheduled tasks" from the chat's menu): pause, edit or delete.
- Turn on notifications in the ChatGPT app to get a push alert when results are ready.

## Gemini

- Open a **normal Gemini chat** (scheduled actions may not run from inside a Gem). Paste the task prompt and add the schedule line, for example: "Do this every weekday at 7:45am."
- Gemini confirms and turns on the scheduled action. Manage it under **Settings and help > Scheduled actions** on the web, or from the profile menu in the app. Up to 10 can be active.
- Some plans prepare results a few hours ahead of the delivery time. If timing matters, schedule a little earlier.

## After it runs

Tell the client what to do with each report:
1. Open the 3 Apply First roles. Save the full job description, not just the link, in a tracker (Teal, a spreadsheet, or Notion).
2. Ask: "Tailor my application for #1" to get the right summary, bullet swaps and an outreach message.
3. Apply to the top 3 the same day while postings are fresh.
4. Once a week, paste in anything they applied to so the task can skip duplicates.
