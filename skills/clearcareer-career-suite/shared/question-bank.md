# The Interview Question Bank

Use these as raw material. Always rephrase to fit what the client actually said and what their resume says. One question per turn.

## Part 1: Rapid Achievement Scan (per role, fast, text mode)

Ask all 8 for the most recent 2 to 3 roles, and Money, Scale and Projects for older roles. Offer the options as a short list the client can answer with a letter or a few words. Word it for their level:
- **Individual contributor:** "your work"
- **Lead / manager / senior:** "your team's work"
- **Executive (VP, Director, Head of, Chief, Founder):** "your decisions"; show the biggest money bands first.

1. **Scale:** Roughly how many people were affected by [your work / your team / your decisions] in this role?
   Under 10 / 10 to 50 / 50 to 250 / 250 to 1,000 / 1,000 to 10,000 / 10,000+ / Help me estimate
2. **Money:** Did your work affect any of these? Revenue, costs, budget, sales, profit, contract value, customer spending, financial risk, none.
   If any: "Ballpark, how much money are we talking?" Under $50K / $50K to $250K / $250K to $1M / $1M to $10M / $10M+ / I know the exact number / Help me estimate
3. **Time:** Did you make anything faster? Processing time, turnaround, manual work you automated, employee hours, launches, hiring, response time.
4. **Growth:** Did anything grow because of you? Revenue, customers, users, conversion, retention, engagement, adoption, market share, pipeline.
5. **Efficiency:** Did anything get better or cheaper? Productivity, cost, errors, quality, capacity, automation, workflow.
6. **People:** Did you manage, hire, train, mentor, build a team, work cross-functionally, manage vendors, or influence executives?
7. **Projects:** Did you launch, build, fix, turn around, migrate, automate, create a new process, implement software, run a transformation, or solve a major problem?
8. **Recognition:** Were you promoted, given an award, top rated, picked for a high-profile project, known as the go-to expert, asked to train others, or handed more responsibility?

## Part 2: Turning scan answers into ranked leads

Every scan answer and resume bullet becomes a "lead" (a possible achievement). Rank leads by these points and interview the highest first:

| Signal | Points |
|---|---|
| Money touched | 30, plus up to 20 more for bigger money bands |
| Growth | 22 |
| Resume bullet that already has a number | 20 |
| Time saved | 18 |
| Efficiency | 18 |
| People | 15 |
| Projects | 12 |
| Recognition | 10 |
| Resume bullet with a trigger verb but no result (led, managed, implemented, launched, built, responsible for, oversaw, owned, created, drove, delivered, onboarded) | 8 |
| Scale band (bigger = more) | up to 40 |

Note for the client when a bullet has no result: "This line describes responsibility without results. That gap is where achievements hide."

## Part 3: The drill-down, per achievement

Work through these five areas. Don't recite them. Pick the one with the biggest gap.

### A. Situation with real texture (the baseline)
"It was slow" is not a baseline. "Each claim took 30 minutes by hand, every claim" is.
- What was it like before you touched it?
- How long did the old way take? How often did it happen?
- What was the workaround people used?
- What did it cost when it went wrong?
- Why did it matter to the business right then?

### B. Scope
- How many people, customers, locations, accounts or systems?
- What was the budget? How long did it run?
- How many departments or teams were involved?
- Was this your region, your company, or bigger?

### C. Action, hyper-specific
Never accept "helped", "assisted", "worked on", "was involved in", "supported" as the final answer.
- What did YOU specifically do? Configure it, write the plan, make the call, train the first group, negotiate the timeline?
- Walk me through it like I'm watching you do it. What's the first thing you actually did?
- What decision did you personally make?
- Who did you have to convince, and how?
- What broke first, and what did you do about it?
- How'd you pull that off?

### D. Result vs. the before
- What changed that someone could see or measure?
- It used to take [before]. What does it take now?
- Who noticed?
- What happened to [revenue / cost / time / errors / satisfaction] afterward?
- Did it stick? Is it still in use?
- What would have happened if you hadn't done it?

### E. Evidence and significance
- How do you know? Did someone report it, measure it, or are we estimating?
- Says who, your boss or the numbers?
- Why did leadership care?
- Did this lead to a promotion, a bigger project, or more responsibility?

## Part 4: Vagueness triggers (always follow up)

| They say | You ask |
|---|---|
| "It was a big project." | What made it big: people, budget, duration, customers, locations, complexity? |
| "A lot of customers." | Let's put a number on "a lot". Closer to 100, 1,000, or 10,000? |
| "It saved significant time." | Let's calculate it. How often did the task happen, and how long did it take before? |
| "It improved performance." | What changed that someone could observe or measure? |
| "It increased engagement." | What did engagement mean here: usage, logins, adoption, participation, retention? |
| "I helped roll it out." | What did you specifically do: configure it, write the plan, make the rollout call, train the first group? |
| "We did really well." | Compared to what: last year, the target, the other teams? |
| "Customers loved it." | How did you know: reviews, NPS, renewals, fewer complaints, referrals? |

## Part 5: Resume phrase triggers

Read every bullet. Each phrase opens a question the client never thought to answer.

| Resume says | Ask about |
|---|---|
| Managed a team of X | Did it grow? Retention? Promotions? Performance vs other teams? Hiring? |
| Implemented [software] | Users? Budget? Adoption rate? Time saved? On time? What did you personally do to drive adoption? |
| Responsible for [area] | What got better while you owned it? What was it like when you started? |
| Led [project] | Budget, team size, duration, deadline, outcome, what would have happened without it |
| Managed [accounts / clients] | How many? Total value? Renewal rate? Growth? Biggest one? |
| Created reports / dashboards | Who used them? How often? What decisions changed? Time saved? |
| Trained staff | How many? What changed after? Ramp time? Errors? |
| Handled customer issues | Volume per day? Resolution time? Satisfaction score? Escalations prevented? |
| Coordinated events | How many? Attendance? Budget? Cost per head? Revenue or leads? |
| Improved processes | Before and after time, errors, cost, people affected |
| Social media / content | Followers before and after, engagement rate, traffic, leads, posts per week |
| Sales / business development | Quota and attainment, rank on the team, deal size, new logos, pipeline |

## Part 6: Hidden-achievement mining prompts

Use these when a role feels thin, or once per role near the end. Many of the best wins aren't on the resume.

1. Tell me about a problem you solved that nobody asked you to solve.
2. Tell me about a time you dealt with a difficult person, customer or stakeholder.
3. Tell me about a mistake or failure, and what you did next.
4. When did you lead something, even without the title?
5. When did you hit a number or beat a target?
6. When did you work under serious pressure or a hard deadline?
7. When did you convince someone to change their mind?
8. When did you learn something fast because you had to?
9. What do people always come to you for?
10. What's a compliment you get over and over at work?
11. What side project or extra responsibility did you pick up?
12. What would break or slow down if you left tomorrow?
13. What are you proud of that never made it onto your resume?
14. What did you build that's still being used?

## Part 8: Role-family probes (metrics that matter most by field)

- **Sales / BD:** quota attainment %, rank on team, revenue closed, deal size, new logos, pipeline built, sales cycle length, win rate
- **Customer success / account management:** accounts and ARR managed, retention and churn, NPS/CSAT, expansion revenue, onboarding time, escalations
- **Marketing:** leads, MQLs and SQLs, CAC, conversion rates, traffic, followers, campaign ROI, budget, content volume
- **Operations / supply chain:** cost per unit, cycle time, on-time %, inventory turns, error rate, vendors, sites
- **Project / program management:** budget, team size, duration, on time and on budget, stakeholders, risks avoided
- **Engineering / IT / data:** uptime, latency, deploy frequency, incidents, users, data volume, cost savings, automation hours saved
- **Finance / accounting:** portfolio or budget size, close time, variance, savings, audit findings, forecast accuracy
- **HR / recruiting / L&D:** hires, time to fill, offer acceptance, retention, people trained, engagement scores
- **Healthcare / education / public sector:** patients or students served, outcomes, compliance, wait times, programs, funding secured
- **Hospitality / retail / service:** customers per shift, average ticket, upsells, reviews, shrink, staff trained, records
- **Executives:** P&L size, headcount, revenue growth, margin, valuation, board-level initiatives, markets entered
