# Setup: ClearCareer Career Clarity (Gemini Gem)

1. In Gemini on the web, open **Gems manager > New Gem** (sometimes under Explore Gems).
2. **Name:** ClearCareer Career Clarity
3. **Description:** Upload your resume and find the 5 job titles you should be targeting, what they pay, and what you actually want next.
4. **Instructions:** paste everything in `gem-instructions.md`.
5. **Knowledge:** upload every file in the `knowledge/` folder (career-suite-guide.md, clarity-framework.md, master-career-profile-template.md, writing-rules.md).
6. **Suggested first messages to share with members:**
   - Here's my resume. What roles should I be targeting?
   - I'm thinking about a career change. Help me get clear.
   - What am I qualified for, and what does it pay?
   - Build my Career Clarity Report
7. Save the Gem. Gemini uses Google Search automatically when needed (this one needs it for live research). Use Canvas for the documents.
8. Test it with a sample resume, then share the Gem link with members.

Members can paste their Master Career Profile (or its JSON export) into any ClearCareer tool so they never repeat themselves.
