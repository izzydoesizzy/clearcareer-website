# The ClearCareer Job Match Rubric (100 points)

Use this to decide which roles a client has the best real chance of landing an interview for. Score on evidence, not keyword overlap. A posting that uses the client's exact words but needs experience they can't prove scores low. A posting with unfamiliar words but a perfect match to proven achievements scores high.

**Do not inflate scores.** A rubric that calls everything 80+ is useless. Most postings a search turns up should land under 75.

## Step 1: Break down the job description

Build this table first:

| Requirement | Type | Priority | Client evidence | Match |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| e.g. Stakeholder management | Required | Critical | Achievement 1.b: aligned 6 departments on rollout | ✅ |

- **Type:** Required, Nice-to-have, or Not mentioned but expected (skills any strong candidate would show).
- **Priority (ATS weight):** Critical = mentioned 3+ times or listed as required. High = mentioned twice or preferred. Medium = mentioned once. Low = implied.
- **Match:** ✅ proven by a specific achievement or role | 🟡 partial, adjacent or only implied | ❌ no evidence.
- Also pull out the **top 3 priorities** of the role: what the hiring manager most needs this person to fix or deliver in year one.

## Step 2: Knockouts (any one = Skip, whatever the score)

- A hard-required license, certification, degree or clearance the client doesn't have
- Work authorization or sponsorship conflict
- Location or onsite requirement that breaks the client's must-have
- Disclosed pay ceiling below the client's floor
- Years of experience far outside the range (for example, asks for 10+ and client has 3, or the reverse for a junior role)
- Any of the client's own dealbreakers
- Posting is closed, expired or a duplicate

List knockouts first. If one hits, stop, label the role **⛔ Skip: [reason]**, and don't score it.

## Step 3: Score

| # | Category | Points | How to score |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Required qualifications proven by achievements | 30 | Share of Required/Critical items marked ✅ (count 🟡 as half), times 30 |
| 2 | Title and seniority fit | 15 | 15 = same level or a natural next step; 10 = one level stretch or adjacent title; 5 = two-level stretch or title change; 0 = step down the client hasn't accepted |
| 3 | Quantified proof for the top 3 priorities | 15 | 5 points per priority with a matching quantified achievement; 2 if matched without a number |
| 4 | Industry and domain fit | 10 | 10 = same industry; 7 = adjacent; 4 = different but skills transfer cleanly; 0 = unrelated and specialized |
| 5 | Nice-to-haves and keyword coverage | 10 | Share of Nice-to-have and High-priority keywords present in the profile, times 10 |
| 6 | Logistics: location, arrangement, pay | 10 | 5 for location/arrangement fit, 5 for pay (5 = meets target, 3 = between floor and target or not disclosed, 0 = below floor) |
| 7 | Values and growth | 5 | Match to the client's must-have values, growth path, company stage |
| 8 | Freshness and access | 5 | 3 for posted within 7 days (1 if 8 to 21 days), plus 2 for a referral path, warm contact, or apply-early signal |

**Notes, not deductions.** Flag likely hiring-manager objections (career gap, short tenures, over or under qualified, career change, industry switch, education) with a one-line strategy. Don't subtract them again: they already show up in categories 1, 2 and 4.

## Step 4: Bands

| Score | Label | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 85 to 100 | 🔥 Apply First | Apply today, tailored, and reach out to a person at the company |
| 75 to 84 | 🟢 Apply Now | Apply this week with a tailored summary and bullet swap |
| 60 to 74 | 🟡 Tailor, then decide | Apply only if you can close a gap with a bullet or a referral |
| Under 60 | ⚪ Skip | Not worth the time unless there's a strategic reason |

## Step 5: Output for each scored role

```
### [Score]/100 [Label]: [Job Title], [Company]
[Location / arrangement] | Posted [date] | [Direct link]
Pay: Base [min to max or Not disclosed] | Bonus/variable [ ] | Equity [ ]

Requirement checklist: (the Step 1 table)

Score card:
Required proof 24/30 | Title fit 15/15 | Top-3 proof 10/15 | Industry 7/10 | Keywords 7/10 | Logistics 8/10 | Values 4/5 | Fresh 3/5

Why you fit: 2 to 4 requirements matched to specific achievements
Your strongest proof: the 2 to 3 bullets to lead with
Gaps: what you don't clearly have, and how to close it (bullet to add or swap, story to prepare)
Likely objection: [one line] / Strategy: [one line]
Summary to use: [which of the 8 summaries, with any tweak]
Verdict: 1 to 2 sentences
```

## Pay rules

Keep pay components separate: base minimum, base maximum, bonus, commission, OTE, equity, signing bonus. Never call OTE, bonus-inclusive or equity-inclusive pay "base salary". If pay isn't posted, say "Not disclosed". Market data is labeled as market data, never as the employer's number.
