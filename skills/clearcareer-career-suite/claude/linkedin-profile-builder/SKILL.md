# LinkedIn Profile Builder

Recruiters search LinkedIn like a database. Your job is to rebuild the client's profile so it shows up for their target titles and proves value in the first 10 seconds, using only facts and numbers from their Master Career Profile.

## Reference files
- `references/linkedin-playbook.md`: formulas, examples and character limits for every section, the checklist and the click paths. **Read before writing.**
- `references/writing-rules.md`: house style and banned words.
- `references/master-career-profile-template.md`: the profile you build from.
- `references/career-suite-guide.md`: the order the tools are used in.

## Step 1: Inputs
1. Ask for the Master Career Profile (or its JSON export). Best results come after the Achievement Interviewer and a Career Clarity Full Report. If they don't have one, work from the resume and the target titles, and tell them the profile will be much stronger once their achievements are quantified.
2. Ask them to paste their current LinkedIn profile, or download it (on LinkedIn: Me > View profile > Resources or More > Save to PDF) and upload it. If they can't, work from the resume.
3. Confirm the top 2 target titles and the city they want to work in.

## Step 2: Score the current profile
Run the checklist from the playbook on what they have now. Show it as a table with ✅ / ❌ and a "before" score out of 12.

## Step 3: Write every section
Create an Artifact (or Doc) titled "LinkedIn Profile: [First Name]" with each section ready to paste, in this order:

1. **Headline:** 10 options, top 3 marked, character count beside each.
2. **About:** the 4-part format with 5 Career Highlights, in two tones (Professional and Conversational), character count for each.
3. **Experience:** an entry for every role, with descriptions for recent roles and the top 3 to 5 bullets underneath.
4. **Skills:** top 5 to pin, plus 15 to 25 more, each linked to a role.
5. **Open to Work settings:** titles, locations, start date, job types, and a one-line recommendation on visibility.
6. **Open to Work post** (only if they're searching openly).
7. **Banner taglines:** 10 options.
8. **Custom URL** suggestion and anything else missing from the checklist (photo, recommendations, featured).

Rules:
- Every number must come from the profile or resume. Never invent one.
- Use the exact title and keyword text recruiters search for the target titles.
- Respect the character limits. Show counts.
- First person in About and Experience, as the playbook says. Zero em dashes, no banned words.

## Step 4: Walk them through pasting it in
Give the click paths from the playbook, one section at a time, starting with the headline (biggest impact). Ask them to say "done" after each one.

## Step 5: After score
Re-run the checklist on the new profile and show the "after" score. List anything still missing (usually photo, banner or recommendations) with one line on how to fix it.

## Step 6: Next
- Save the headline and About summary to the Master Career Profile.
- Next step from the guide: "Set up your job alerts and daily job search autopilot so the right roles come to you."
- Suggest updating the headline again if their targets change (after a Career Clarity Refresh).
