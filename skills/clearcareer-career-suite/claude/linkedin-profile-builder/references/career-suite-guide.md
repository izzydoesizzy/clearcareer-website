# The ClearCareer Career Suite: Your Step-by-Step Guide

Six AI tools, used in a set order. Each one builds on the last, and they all share one document: your **Master Career Profile**. Follow the steps below and you'll go from "here's my resume" to "here are the 3 jobs I should apply to today", with every achievement backed by a number.

## Why the order matters

There's a chicken-and-egg problem in every job search. A weak resume hides where you fit, so it's hard to pick the right job titles. But you can't write a strong resume without knowing what it's for.

So we do clarity twice:
1. A **quick pass** to get working titles,
2. then the **achievement interview** puts real numbers on your experience,
3. then a **full pass** with that evidence, which gives sharper titles, real pay numbers and a company list.

## The roadmap

| Step | Tool | What you do | What you get | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Career Clarity: Quick Start** | Upload your resume, answer 5 short questions | 3 to 5 working job titles, rough pay, honest feedback on your resume | 10 to 15 min |
| 2 | **AI Achievement Interviewer** | Quick scan in text, then talk it through in voice mode, role by role | Your Master Career Profile with a number on every achievement, new resume bullets, 8 professional summaries | 2 to 3 voice sittings of 20 to 45 min |
| 3 | **Career Clarity: Full Report** | Bring your Master Career Profile back to Career Clarity. Turn on deep research for pay. | Final 5 titles, pay for your city with sources, 20 to 30 target companies, a reality check | 30 to 45 min |
| 4 | **AI Achievement Interviewer: Summary Refresh** | Say "refresh my summaries" with your final titles | Summaries and bullet order tuned to your final titles. Your resume is done. | 10 min |
| 5 | **LinkedIn Profile Builder** | Paste your current LinkedIn and your profile | Headline, About, experience entries, skills, Open to Work settings, all ready to paste | 30 to 45 min |
| 6 | **Job Alerts Setup** | Follow the click-by-click steps | LinkedIn, Google and Indeed alerts for your titles and companies | 20 min |
| 7 | **Job Search Autopilot** | Confirm your search plan, then schedule the prompts | A daily scout that finds, checks and scores new jobs, plus weekly sweeps | 15 min |
| 8 | **Job Match Scorer** (ongoing) | Paste any job you're thinking about | A score out of 100, the proof to lead with, gaps, and a tailored summary | 2 min per job |

**Week one:** steps 1 to 4 (your resume is done).
**Week two:** steps 5 to 7 (your search runs itself).
**Every day after that:** step 8 on the roles the autopilot surfaces, and apply to the top 3.

## Where should you start?

| Your situation | Start at | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Most people: not sure the resume is working, not 100% sure on titles | Step 1 | Follow the roadmap as written |
| Resume is mostly duties ("responsible for", "helped with"), few numbers | Step 1 | Keep the Quick Start short. The interview is where the real work happens. |
| Strong resume (most bullets have numbers) and you know your target titles | Step 3 (Full Report) | Then a short interview round on any role that's thin |
| Changing careers | Step 1 | Do the full intake in Quick Start (values, keep / drop / build skills). Use the "test before you commit" ideas before going all in. |
| Already have a profile, search has been quiet for 4+ weeks | Career Clarity: Refresh | Then re-run steps 4 to 7 with the new targets |
| Just need LinkedIn fixed | Step 5 | It works from your resume, but it's much better with a profile |
| Just want to know if you fit one job | Step 8 | Works from your resume alone. Scores get sharper with a profile. |

## How the tools share information

Your **Master Career Profile** is the thread. Every tool reads it and adds to it.

- **In Claude:** keep it as an artifact. In a new chat, paste it in or upload it.
- **In ChatGPT and Gemini:** each tool is its own GPT or Gem. Copy the profile (or just the JSON block at the bottom) and paste it into the next one.
- **Save a copy after every step.** A Google Doc, a Word doc, or a note. It's yours.

Starting a new tool? Paste your profile first and say what you want. Example: "Here's my Master Career Profile. Build my LinkedIn profile."

## Voice mode tips (step 2)

- Use your phone. Voice works best in the mobile apps.
- Find a quiet 30 minutes. Walking works great.
- "I don't know" is fine. The interviewer will help you get to a number you can stand behind.
- When you finish a voice sitting, go back to typing and say "rebuild my profile".
- Keep going until your Quantification Coverage is 80% or higher.

## Which platform should you use?

All six tools work in Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini. Pick the one you already pay for.

| | Claude | ChatGPT | Gemini |
|---|---|---|---|
| How the tools install | Skills (one folder each) | One Custom GPT per tool | One Gem per tool |
| Living documents | Artifacts | Canvas | Canvas or Google Docs |
| Voice interview | Voice mode in the mobile app | Voice in the mobile app | Gemini Live |
| Deep research for pay | Research mode | Deep research | Deep Research |
| Scheduled job searches | Scheduled tasks (paid plans) | Scheduled tasks in a normal chat (not inside the GPT) | Scheduled actions in a normal chat |

If you have a choice: Claude keeps everything in one place most easily, since skills can all run in the same conversation.
