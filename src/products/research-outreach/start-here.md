# Start here: Research and Outreach Kit

Welcome! This kit is for anyone who's tired of scrolling job boards, applying to everything and hearing nothing back. It's built from the same tools I use with the people I coach, and it works in three moves. First, find fresh jobs from the last 7 days and score each one out of 12, so your time goes to the roles that fit. Second, research and rank your target companies, so you know where to aim first. Third, reach the people who actually hire with short, specific emails that could only have been written to them. You'll get four guides you can use by hand and three Claude skills that do the heavy lifting with you.

## What's inside

- **The Fit Score:** how to score any job out of 12 on six parts (skills, experience, industry, location, growth and pay), with a worksheet and a 10-posting tracker.
- **Company Scorecards:** the method for scoring companies on culture, growth, open roles, pay and industry, weighted to your priorities, with fill-in templates.
- **The Cold Outreach Campaign:** the method end to end: find one person, work out their email, find a hook, write the email, follow up and track it, with the email rules and examples.
- **Search Tools:** Boolean search strings for jobs, recruiters and hiring managers, 20 Google Alerts in 5 categories, a daily action plan and a way to find events and associations.
- **The cc-job-scan Claude skill:** finds postings from the last 7 days, checks the links, scores each role out of 12 and gives you a ranked table, a card for each job and a pattern note on what employers keep asking for.
- **The cc-company-scorecards Claude skill:** researches 10 to 20 companies with sources, scores them, applies your weights and ranks them into tiers with a top 3. It can build your target list from scratch, too.
- **The cc-outreach-campaign Claude skill:** for your top companies, finds one publicly listed person, suggests how to work out their email, finds something real they've done, and writes a 75 to 125 word email in your voice, plus a follow-up and a tracker.

## Your first 10 minutes

1. **Find one job posting** you've been thinking about. Check it was posted in the last 7 days and that the link still works. (2 minutes)
2. **Score it out of 12** with the worksheet in The Fit Score. Be honest. (4 minutes)
3. **If it's 9 or more,** find the person who'd be your boss: search the company's LinkedIn page, click People, and type the function plus "director" or "manager". Write down their name. (3 minutes)
4. **Write one line** about something specific that person has posted or worked on. Tomorrow, that line becomes the opening of your email. (1 minute)

That's your first win: one real job, one honest score and one real person to write to.

## Using the Claude skills

To install the skills, open the **Install** tab on this page and follow the steps there. Once they're installed, start a new chat in Claude and ask in plain words. Try one of these:

- "Find senior project manager jobs in Toronto or remote from the last week and score them against my resume."
- "Score this job post out of 12 against my resume." (then paste it)
- "Score these 12 companies for me. I care most about remote work and growth."
- "I'm a payroll specialist in Calgary. Build me a target company list."
- "Here's my resume and my top 10 companies. Build my outreach campaign."
- "Who should I email about a data analyst role at [Company], and how do I work out their email?"

Each skill asks for anything it's missing. The job scan and company research work best when Claude can search the web. If it can't, paste in what you've found and the skill will score and organize it. The skills never invent jobs, companies, people or email addresses. A guessed email is always marked as a guess, and every hook comes with a link.

## No Claude? Use any AI

1. **Open the Copy-paste prompts tab** on this page and pick the prompt you need: scoring a job, finding fresh postings, scoring companies, finding the right person or writing an email.
2. **Paste it into any AI chat tool** and swap everything in [square brackets] for your own details. For multi-step prompts, run Step 1, read the result, then paste Step 2 in the same chat.
3. **Check everything yourself.** Click every link, confirm every name and title, verify every email address, and fix anything that isn't true before you send or apply.

## A 7-day plan

- **Day 1:** Read The Fit Score. Score 5 postings from the last 7 days.
- **Day 2:** Set up your search strings and 15 to 20 Google Alerts from Search Tools. Score 5 more postings.
- **Day 3:** Pick 10 to 20 target companies and set your weights. Start your scorecards.
- **Day 4:** Finish your scorecards. Rank them and pick your top 10 for outreach.
- **Day 5:** Find one person at each of your top 10, work out their emails and find a hook for each.
- **Day 6:** Write and send your first 3 emails. Apply to every role that scored APPLY.
- **Day 7:** Send 2 or 3 more, put every follow-up date in your calendar, and rest.

## Good to know

- **Results vary.** A fit score is a guide, not a prediction, and a great email doesn't guarantee a reply. Count what you control: postings scored, companies researched, emails sent, follow-ups done.
- **Keep your facts true.** Use only your real skills, wins and numbers. Never claim a connection you don't have. People check.
- **Check before you act.** Job postings close, people change roles and company news goes stale. Open every link and confirm every name before you apply or send.
- **One person at a time.** These are personal, one-to-one emails, not marketing. Follow the spirit of Canada's anti-spam law (CASL) and the US CAN-SPAM Act: use your real name and contact details, never add anyone to a list, and if someone asks you to stop, stop. This is general guidance, not legal or financial advice.
