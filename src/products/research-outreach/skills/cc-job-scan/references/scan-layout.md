# Job scan layout

Read this before you put the job scan together. Use this layout, in this order. Keep it scannable: tables first, short sentences, every link clickable. Swap the [brackets] for real values and leave out any section that has nothing in it (except the summary table).

**1. Header**

~~~text
# Job scan: [Target role] in [Location]

Scanned: [Today's date]
Postings found: [N] from the last 7 days ([N] removed as old, closed or duplicate)
Titles searched: [Title 1], [Title 2], [Title 3]
Where I looked: [LinkedIn Jobs, company careers pages, application boards, job boards]
Links: [Every link was checked on [date] / Some links couldn't be checked, see each card / I couldn't open web pages, so please click each link before applying]
~~~

**2. Summary table**

All roles, ranked by score. Use short recency labels: "Today", "1d ago", "3d ago", or "Date not shown".

~~~text
## Summary: [N] roles ranked by fit

| # | Company | Role | Posted | Score | Call | Link |
|---|---------|------|--------|-------|------|------|
| 1 | [Company] | [Job title] | 2d ago | 10/12 | APPLY | [View posting](URL) |
| 2 | [Company] | [Job title] | Today | 9/12 | APPLY | [View posting](URL) |
| 3 | [Company] | [Job title] | 5d ago | 7/12 | CONSIDER | [View posting](URL) |

Key: APPLY (9 to 12) = strong match, go first. CONSIDER (6 to 8) = worth applying, some gaps. SKIP (0 to 5) = weak match.
~~~

**3. Job cards**

One card per role, in the same order as the table.

~~~text

[View posting](URL) ([Link checked [date] / Couldn't check / Not checked])

| Part | Score | Why |
|------|-------|-----|
| Skills Match | [0 to 2]/2 | [One sentence, with the count] |

| Industry Fit | [0 to 2]/2 | [One sentence] |

**Gaps and concerns:** [1 to 2 sentences, or "None found".]

~~~

**4. Pattern note**

Count only from the postings in this scan. Show counts as "X of N postings".

~~~text

- [Skill, tool or certification]: [X] of [N] postings

- [Requirement] ([X] of [N] postings). If you have it, add it to your resume. If you don't, it's a good thing to learn next.

Work model: [N] remote, [N] hybrid, [N] on-site.
Companies with more than one opening: [Company] ([N] roles), or "None".
Other titles for this work: [Title], [Title].
~~~

**6. What I removed**

~~~text
## Removed

- [Job title] at [Company]: posted [date], older than 7 days
- [Job title] at [Company]: the page says the job is closed
- [Job title] at [Company]: duplicate of #[N]
~~~

**Writing rules for the scan**

- No em dashes or en dashes. Write ranges with "to" ("$80,000 to $95,000 CAD").
- Use the user's spelling (Canadian or US).
- Every posting in the scan has its own link. No link, no card.
- Don't add salary estimates, company facts or claims the postings don't support.
- End with one next step, then the attribution line from the skill.
