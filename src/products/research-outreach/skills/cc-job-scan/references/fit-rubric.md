# The fit score: 6 parts, out of 12

Read this before you score any posting. Each part gets 0, 1 or 2. No half points. Add the six parts for a total out of 12, then make the call.

Score honestly. An inflated score sends the user after the wrong jobs and costs them time they don't have. Score from two things only: the posting, and the user's real background (their resume and what they've told you).

**Skills Match**

- 2: 80% or more of the required skills match the user's documented skills and experience
- 1: 50% to 79% match
- 0: under 50% match

How to work it out: list the required skills (not the "nice to haves"). Tick the ones the user's resume or messages show. Divide, and show the count in your reasoning: "5 of 6 required skills (83%)". If the user might have a skill that isn't on the resume, don't give credit. Ask: "Do you have Workday experience? If yes, Skills Match goes from 1 to 2."

**Experience Level**

- 2: the seniority matches exactly (years and scope line up)
- 1: one level up or down (for example, a senior person applying for a lead role)
- 0: two or more levels off

Look at the years asked for, the title level, and the scope (team size, budget, who they report to). If the posting doesn't say, score from what it does say and note it.

**Industry Fit**

- 2: the user has direct experience in this industry
- 1: an adjacent industry with knowledge that transfers
- 0: no industry overlap

**Location / Remote**

- 2: matches the user's location preference exactly
- 1: a partial match (hybrid when they want remote, more office days than they want, or a nearby city)
- 0: needs a move, or contradicts what they want

A "remote" role that only hires in another country scores 0, unless the user can already work there.

**Growth Potential**

- 2: a clear path to the next-level role the user wants
- 1: a lateral move that builds new, useful skills
- 0: a dead end, a step down, or no career upside

**Compensation**

- 2: meets or beats the user's target salary
- 1: within 15% below the target, or pay not listed
- 0: more than 15% below the target

How to work it out: if the user's target sits inside the posted range, or below it, score 2. If the top of the range is within 15% below the target, score 1. If the top of the range is more than 15% below, score 0. Show the maths: a $105,000 target means 15% below is $89,250. If pay isn't listed, score 1 and write "Not listed". If the user has no target, score 1 and write "No target set". If the posting is in a different currency from the target, say so, and label any conversion "(estimate, confirm)".

**The call**

- **APPLY (9 to 12):** a strong match. Go first.
- **CONSIDER (6 to 8):** worth applying, with some gaps to address.
- **SKIP (0 to 5):** a weak match. Spend the time elsewhere.

Break ties by the most recently posted first, then by the higher Skills Match.

**What goes with every score**

- **Reasoning:** one specific sentence per part. Name the skill, the level, the city or the number.
- **Why this role fits:** 2 to 3 sentences on why it matches the user. If it was posted in the last 1 or 2 days, say so.
- **Gaps and concerns:** 1 to 2 sentences, or "None found".
- **Questions:** anything the user could answer that would change a score.

**A worked example (fictional)**

Sam is a project manager in Toronto with 7 years running projects at a hospital network. Sam holds a PMP, wants a Senior Project Manager role (hybrid, up to 2 office days a week, or remote) and has a target salary of $105,000 CAD.

The posting: Senior Project Manager at Lumen Health (a fictional company) in Mississauga, Ontario. Hybrid, 4 days a week in the office. $96,000 to $110,000 CAD. Posted 2 days ago. Required: 5+ years managing projects, PMP, budget management, vendor management, electronic health record (EHR) implementation, Agile delivery.

~~~text
Senior Project Manager at Lumen Health
Posted 2d ago | 10/12 APPLY | Hybrid | $96,000 to $110,000 CAD
Link checked [date]

Part              | Score | Why
Skills Match      | 1/2   | 4 of 6 required skills (67%). No EHR implementation or Agile delivery on the resume.
Experience Level  | 2/2   | 7 years against 5+ asked, at the same project scope.
Industry Fit      | 2/2   | Direct health care experience at a hospital network.
Location / Remote | 1/2   | Mississauga is close to Toronto, but 4 office days is more than the 2 Sam wants.
Growth Potential  | 2/2   | The senior title is the next step Sam is aiming for.
Compensation      | 2/2   | The $105,000 target sits inside the posted range.

Why this role fits: Posted 2 days ago, so it's fresh. Sam's 7 years of hospital projects and PMP line up with the core of the role, and the senior title is the step Sam wants.

Gaps and concerns: No EHR implementation or Agile delivery on the resume. Four office days a week is more than Sam wants, so ask about flexibility early.

Questions: Have you worked on any clinical software rollout, even in a support role? If yes, Skills Match could go to 2.
~~~

## Score a job post out of 12

**What it does:** Scores one job posting against your background on 6 parts and tells you whether to apply, consider it or skip it.
**When to use it:** You found a posting and want an honest read before you spend an hour on the application.

### Step 1: Score it

~~~text
Score this job posting against my background using the fit rubric below. Be honest. Don't inflate scores to make me feel good.

My resume: [Paste your resume]
My target role: [Target job title]
My location and work preference: [City, province or state, and remote, hybrid or on-site]
My target salary: [Amount and currency, or "not set"]
The next step I want in my career: [The next role you're aiming for]

The posting:
[Paste the job posting]

RUBRIC (each part scores 0, 1 or 2, for a total out of 12)
Skills Match: 2 = 80% or more of the required skills match my documented skills and experience. 1 = 50% to 79%. 0 = under 50%.
Experience Level: 2 = the seniority matches exactly (years and scope). 1 = one level up or down. 0 = two or more levels off.
Industry Fit: 2 = I have direct experience in this industry. 1 = an adjacent industry with knowledge that transfers. 0 = no overlap.
Location / Remote: 2 = matches my preference exactly. 1 = a partial match (hybrid when I want remote, or a nearby city). 0 = needs a move or contradicts my preference.
Growth Potential: 2 = a clear path to the next-level role I want. 1 = a lateral move that builds new, useful skills. 0 = a dead end, a step down or no upside.
Compensation: 2 = meets or beats my target. 1 = within 15% below my target, or pay not listed. 0 = more than 15% below my target.

Rules:
- Use only 0, 1 or 2. Use only facts from my resume and the posting.
- For Skills Match, list the required skills and show how many I have, for example "5 of 7 (71%)".
- If you aren't sure I have a skill, don't give me credit. Ask me instead.

Output:
1. A table: Part | Score | Why (one specific sentence each)
2. My total out of 12 and the call: APPLY (9 to 12), CONSIDER (6 to 8) or SKIP (0 to 5)
3. Why this role fits me (2 to 3 sentences)
4. Gaps and concerns (1 to 2 sentences, or "None found")
5. Any questions that could change a score
~~~

**Replace:** [Paste your resume], [Target job title], [City, province or state, and remote, hybrid or on-site], [Amount and currency, or "not set"], [The next role you're aiming for], [Paste the job posting]

## Score and rank a batch of postings

**What it does:** Scores several postings with the same rubric, ranks them in one table, then shows you the patterns across them.
**When to use it:** You've saved a pile of postings and need to decide which ones get your time this week.

### Step 1: Score and rank them

Score each job posting below against my background, then rank them. Be honest. Don't inflate scores.

My resume: [Paste your resume]
My target role: [Target job title]
My location and work preference: [City, province or state, and remote, hybrid or on-site]
My target salary: [Amount and currency, or "not set"]
The next step I want in my career: [The next role you're aiming for]

RUBRIC (each part scores 0, 1 or 2, for a total out of 12)
Skills Match: 2 = 80% or more of the required skills match. 1 = 50% to 79%. 0 = under 50%.
Experience Level: 2 = seniority matches exactly. 1 = one level up or down. 0 = two or more levels off.
Industry Fit: 2 = direct experience. 1 = adjacent industry. 0 = no overlap.
Location / Remote: 2 = matches exactly. 1 = partial match. 0 = needs a move or contradicts my preference.
Growth Potential: 2 = clear path to the role I want next. 1 = lateral move with new skills. 0 = dead end or step down.
Compensation: 2 = meets or beats my target. 1 = within 15% below, or not listed. 0 = more than 15% below.

The postings (each one starts with a line that says NEXT POSTING):
[Paste the postings]

Output:
1. A summary table ranked by score: # | Company | Role | Posted | Score | Call | Link
   Calls: APPLY (9 to 12), CONSIDER (6 to 8), SKIP (0 to 5). Break ties by the most recently posted, then by Skills Match.
2. A card for each posting: the six scores with one sentence each, why it fits (2 to 3 sentences) and gaps and concerns (1 to 2 sentences).
Use only facts from my resume and the postings. If you aren't sure I have a skill, ask instead of giving credit.
~~~

### Step 2: Spot the patterns

~~~text
Now look across all the postings and give me a pattern note:
1. Common requirements: skills, tools, certifications and experience that show up in 3 or more postings, with the count (for example "Agile: 6 of 9 postings").
2. Skills gaps: requirements that show up often but aren't on my resume. For each one, ask me whether I have it and left it off (then I should add it) or don't have it yet (then it's something to learn).
3. Pay: the ranges you saw, with currency, and how many postings listed pay at all.
4. Work model mix: how many were remote, hybrid and on-site.
5. Title variants: other job titles you saw for the same kind of work.
Count only what's in these postings. Don't present anything as a market-wide statistic.
~~~

**Replace:** nothing. Use it in the same chat, right after Step 1.
