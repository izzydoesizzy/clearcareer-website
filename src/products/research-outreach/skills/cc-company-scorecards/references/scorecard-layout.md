# Scorecard layout

Read this before you put the scorecards together. Use this layout, in this order. Tables first, short sentences, a link for every fact. Swap the [brackets] for real values. Leave out any section that has nothing in it.

**1. Header and weights**

~~~text
# Company scorecards: [Target role]

Researched: [Today's date]
Companies scored: [N]
Your weights: Culture [3], Growth [3], Roles [2], Pay [2], Industry [1] (3 = top priority, 2 = important, 1 = nice to have). Want different weights? Tell me and I'll re-rank.
Dealbreakers: [The user's dealbreakers, or "None"]
~~~

**2. Ranked table**

~~~text
## Ranked by weighted score

| Rank | Company | Culture | Growth | Roles | Pay | Industry | Total /50 | Weighted /10 | Evidence | Review rating | Flag |
|------|---------|---------|--------|-------|-----|----------|-----------|--------------|----------|---------------|------|
| 1 | [Company] | 9 | 8 | 9 | 7 | 8 | 41 | 8.3 | Strong | [Rating, site, date] | |
| 2 | [Company] | 8 | 9 | 7 | 6 | 6 | 36 | 7.5 | Some | Not found | |
| 3 | [Company] | 6 | 5 | 9 | 9 | 8 | 37 | 7.0 | Some | [Rating, site, date] | DEALBREAKER: [why] |
~~~

**3. A scorecard for each company**

In rank order.

~~~text
### [Rank]. [Company] ([Weighted score]/10, [Tier])

**Key insight:** [One sentence on why this company is a good or poor fit for the user.]

| Area | Score | Why | Source |
|------|-------|-----|--------|
| Culture fit | [1 to 10] | [One line] | [Link, date] |
| Growth | [1 to 10] | [One line] | [Link, date] |
| Open roles | [1 to 10] | [One line] | [Link, date] |
| Pay | [1 to 10] | [One line, with currency] | [Link, date] |
| Industry position | [1 to 10] | [One line] | [Link, date] |

**Open roles that match:** [Job title](link), posted [date]. Or "None right now".
**Size and location:** [Size], [Location and office policy]
**Watch-outs:** [Anything that could be a problem, or "None found"]
**Best way in:** [Apply to a role, or reach out to the leader of a team]
**Evidence:** [Strong, Some or Thin]
~~~

**4. Tiers**

~~~text
## Your tiers

**Strong fit (8.0 and up):** [Company], [Company]
**Good fit (6.0 to 7.9):** [Company], [Company], [Company]
**Worth watching (under 6.0):** [Company], [Company]
~~~

**5. Top 3 to go after first**

~~~text
## Where to start

1. **[Company]:** [One or two sentences on why, and the first move.]
2. **[Company]:** [One or two sentences on why, and the first move.]
3. **[Company]:** [One or two sentences on why, and the first move.]

Before you act, check: [Anything time-sensitive, such as an open role or recent news.]
~~~

If pay data appears anywhere, add this line under the ranked table: "This is general guidance, not legal or financial advice."

**Writing rules**

- No em dashes or en dashes. Write ranges with "to".
- Use the user's spelling (Canadian or US).
- Every fact has a source and a date, or says "Not found".
- End with one next step, then the attribution line from the skill.
