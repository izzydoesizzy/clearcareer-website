# Start here: The Resume and LinkedIn Audit Kit

Welcome in. This kit is how I review resumes and LinkedIn profiles, turned into something you can run on your own. I go area by area with a checklist, give each area a verdict, and back every verdict with evidence from your actual documents. No generic advice. You get two Claude skills that do this for your resume and your LinkedIn profile, at the depth you pick: a quick Snapshot, a Full audit, or a Rewrite Guide that quotes your lines and rewrites them using only your real facts. You also get the checklists themselves, so you can score yourself with a pen if you'd rather.

## What's inside

**Guides (read on this page):**
- **01 How the Audits Work:** the four verdicts, the three depths, what to upload, how to take screenshots and save your LinkedIn PDF, how to use your results, and the re-audit routine.
- **02 Resume Checklist:** all 30 resume checks and the verdict rules, plus four quick tests and a scorecard. No AI needed.
- **03 LinkedIn Checklist:** all 24 LinkedIn checks and the verdict rules, plus four quick tests and a scorecard.

**Claude skills:**
- **cc-resume-review:** audits your resume across 6 areas (first impressions, story, achievements, keywords, career path, language) against your target role or a job post.
- **cc-linkedin-audit:** audits your photo, banner, headline, About section and Experience from screenshots, a PDF export or pasted text.
- **Their prompt cards:** the rubrics and every audit format are also copy-paste prompts, so you can run the same audits in any AI tool.

## Your first 10 minutes

1. Open **Guide 02** and do the four quick tests on your resume (the glance test, the highlighter test, the first-word test and the stranger test). It takes about 5 minutes.
2. Install the resume skill (see below), or open the resume prompt cards on this page.
3. Upload your resume as a PDF, tell it the role you're going for, and ask for a **Snapshot**.
4. Read the bold lines and the verdict table. Then make the one fix it gives you under "Your first fix".

That's it. You now know where your resume stands and you've already made it better.

## Using the Claude skills

To install the skills, open the **Install** tab on this page and follow the steps. It takes a few minutes, and you only do it once.

Once they're installed, just talk to Claude normally. Try one of these:

- "Here's my resume. I'm going for operations manager roles. Do a full audit."
- "Snapshot my resume against this job post." (then paste the posting)
- "Give me the Rewrite Guide for my resume. Quote my lines and fix them."
- "Here are screenshots of my LinkedIn profile. Do a full audit. I'm going for UX research roles."
- "Rewrite my LinkedIn headline, About and Experience. Here's my PDF export and my resume."
- "I updated my resume. Re-audit it and show me what changed."

If you don't say which depth you want, you'll get the Full audit. The skills never invent facts. When a rewrite needs a number you haven't given, you'll see **[add a number]** and a question to help you find it.

For your LinkedIn audit, send screenshots if you can. Your photo and banner can only be checked from a screenshot. Guide 01 shows you how to take them, and how to save your profile as a PDF (on a computer: your profile, **More**, **Save to PDF**).

## No Claude? Use any AI

1. Open the prompt cards on this page and copy the **rubric** prompt for your resume or your LinkedIn. Paste it into a new chat in the AI assistant you already use.
2. Copy the prompt for the depth you want (Snapshot, Full audit, Rewrite Guide or Re-audit), replace everything in [square brackets], and paste it in with your resume, PDF or screenshots.
3. Check every fact in the answer before you use it. If it added a number, a tool or a title you didn't give it, delete it.

For photo and banner checks, use an AI tool that can read images.

## A 7-day plan

- **Day 1:** Do the four quick tests in Guide 02, then run a Snapshot of your resume.
- **Day 2:** Run a Full audit of your resume with a real job post. Fix the "Fix these first" list.
- **Day 3:** Run the Rewrite Guide on your resume. Find your numbers for every [add a number] and send them back.
- **Day 4:** Update your resume, save it as a new version, and run a re-audit.
- **Day 5:** Take screenshots of your LinkedIn profile (Guide 01) and run a Full audit.
- **Day 6:** Run the LinkedIn Rewrite Guide. Update your headline and About section.
- **Day 7:** Update your 3 most recent roles on LinkedIn, check your titles and dates match your resume, and re-audit both.

Short on time? Do Days 2, 3 and 6. That covers the biggest fixes on both.

## Good to know

- **Results vary.** These audits use the same checklist I use, but a strong resume and profile can't guarantee interviews or offers. Hiring depends on a lot you can't control.
- **Keep your facts true.** Every number, title and date should be something you can explain if someone asks. If you estimate a number, know how you got it.
- **The verdicts are a guide.** They come from a fixed rubric, so they're consistent. You know your field. If something doesn't fit, trust that.
- **Your privacy.** You can remove your phone number and address before you upload. The audits don't need them.
- **Need help?** Reply to your purchase email and it comes straight to me.

One good fix today beats a perfect rewrite next month. Start with the Snapshot.
