# Start here: the ClearCareer Career Suite

Hey, I'm Izzy. These 6 tools take you from "here's my resume" to "here are the jobs I should apply to today", with numbers on your work along the way. They work in Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini.

## Why the order matters

A weak resume hides where you fit, so it's hard to pick the right titles. But you can't write a strong resume without knowing what it's for. So you do clarity twice: a quick pass to get working titles, then the interview puts numbers on your experience, then a full pass with that evidence.

## The steps, in order

1. **Career Clarity: Quick Start** (10 to 15 minutes). Upload your resume and answer 5 short questions. You get working titles, rough pay and honest resume feedback.
2. **AI Achievement Interviewer** (2 or 3 sittings of 20 to 45 minutes). A quick text scan, then a voice interview that digs into every role until it finds the numbers. It writes your bullets, 8 professional summaries, headlines and a skills section.
3. **Career Clarity: Full Report** (30 to 45 minutes). Bring your Master Career Profile back. Turn on deep research if your AI has it. You get your final 5 titles, pay for your city with a source for every figure, and 20 to 30 companies to target.
4. **Summary refresh** (10 minutes). Tell the Achievement Interviewer "refresh my summaries" and your resume is done.
5. **LinkedIn Profile Builder** (30 to 45 minutes). Headline, About, experience, skills and Open to Work settings, written from your numbers and ready to paste.
6. **Job Alerts Setup** (20 minutes). Copy-paste alerts for LinkedIn, Google and Indeed, with click-by-click steps.
7. **Job Search Autopilot** (15 minutes). Scheduled searches that find, check and score new roles every weekday.
8. **Job Match Scorer** (2 minutes a job, ongoing). Paste any job post. You get a score out of 100 on your real chances, the proof to lead with, your gaps, and a summary written for that job.

Strong resume and you already know your titles? Start at step 3. Search has been quiet for 4+ weeks? Run Career Clarity again and ask for a Refresh.

## The thread: your Master Career Profile

The Achievement Interviewer keeps one living document with every role, win and number, plus the reasoning behind each number. Save a copy after every step. Paste it into any of the 6 tools (or any of my other skills) and you never repeat yourself.

When you don't know a number, the interviewer doesn't let it go. It works down a ladder (exact, rough, a range, work it out, then the safe lower number) until you have one you can stand behind. The reasoning goes in your profile, so you can explain it if an interviewer asks.

## Install in Claude

Open **Claude skills** on this page and download each zip. In Claude, go to Settings, then Capabilities, then Skills, and upload them one at a time. The **Install** tab has the full steps, plus Claude Code and Claude Projects.

Then try: "Use career-clarity to run my Quick Start." and attach your resume.

## Use it in ChatGPT or Gemini

Download the **ChatGPT version** or the **Gemini version** from the Downloads list on this page. Each folder inside is one tool.

1. Open the folder's `setup.md` and follow it. In ChatGPT you make a Custom GPT. In Gemini you make a Gem.
2. Paste the instructions file, and upload everything in the `knowledge` folder.

Each tool's knowledge files include the full step-by-step guide, so any of them can tell you what comes next.

## Your first two weeks

- **Day 1:** Career Clarity Quick Start. Pick 2 or 3 working titles.
- **Days 2 to 4:** The Achievement Interviewer, one role per sitting. Use voice if you can. It's faster and you'll remember more.
- **Day 5:** Career Clarity Full Report, then the summary refresh. Your resume is done.
- **Week 2:** LinkedIn Profile Builder, then Job Alerts Setup and Job Search Autopilot.
- **Every day after:** Job Match Scorer on what the autopilot finds. Apply to the top 3.

In Land and Level Up? Bring what you find to Wednesday's call. That's what the room is for.
