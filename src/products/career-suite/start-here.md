# Start here: the ClearCareer Career Suite

Hey, I'm Izzy. These 5 tools take you from "here's my resume" to "here are the jobs I should apply to today", with numbers on your work along the way. They're for Land and Level Up members, and they work in Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini.

## The 5 tools, in order

1. **Career Clarity** (15 to 20 minutes). Upload your resume and answer a short intake. You get 5 target titles with fit scores, what they pay where you live, your negotiation numbers, a reality check and resume feedback. Turn on deep research if your AI has it: every pay figure comes with a link to its source.
2. **AI Achievement Interviewer** (2 or 3 sittings of 20 to 45 minutes). A quick text scan, then a voice interview that digs into every role until it finds the numbers. It writes your bullets, 8 professional summaries, headlines and a skills section.
3. **Job Match Scorer** (2 minutes a job). Paste any job post. You get a score out of 100 on your real chances, the proof to lead with, your gaps, and a summary written for that job.
4. **Job Search Autopilot** (15 minutes to set up). It turns your profile into scheduled searches that find, check and score new roles for you every weekday.
5. **Job Alerts Setup** (20 minutes to set up). Copy-paste alerts for LinkedIn, Google and Indeed, built from your titles, with click-by-click steps.

## The thread: your Master Career Profile

The Achievement Interviewer keeps one living document with every role, win and number, plus the reasoning behind each number. Save it. Paste it into any of the 5 tools (or any of my other skills) and you never repeat yourself.

When you don't know a number, the interviewer doesn't let it go. It works down a ladder (exact, rough, a range, work it out, then the safe lower number) until you have one you can stand behind. The reasoning goes in your profile, so you can explain it if an interviewer asks.

## Install in Claude

Open **Claude skills** on this page and download each zip. In Claude, go to Settings, then Capabilities, then Skills, and upload them one at a time. The **Install** tab has the full steps, plus Claude Code and Claude Projects.

Then try: "Use career-clarity to build my Career Clarity Report." and attach your resume.

## Use it in ChatGPT or Gemini

Download the **ChatGPT version** or the **Gemini version** from the Downloads list on this page. Each folder inside is one tool.

1. Open the folder's `setup.md` and follow it. In ChatGPT you make a Custom GPT. In Gemini you make a Gem.
2. Paste the instructions file, and upload everything in the `knowledge` folder.
3. Skip the last step about sharing the link. That one is for me. Yours is just for you.

## Your first week

- **Day 1:** Career Clarity. Pick the 2 or 3 titles you'll go after.
- **Days 2 to 4:** The Achievement Interviewer, one role per sitting. Use voice if you can. It's faster and you'll remember more.
- **Day 5:** Job Match Scorer on 3 real job posts. Notice which proof keeps coming up.
- **Day 6:** Job Alerts Setup, then Job Search Autopilot.
- **Day 7:** Rest. The autopilot works while you do.

Bring what you find to Wednesday's call. That's what the room is for.
