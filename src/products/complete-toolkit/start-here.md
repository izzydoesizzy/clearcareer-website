# Start here: The Complete Job Search Toolkit

Welcome in. This is everything in one place: five products that cover a job search from "What am I even aiming for?" to "I'm happy to accept." You'll use them in order, one stage at a time, so you're never staring at a pile of tools wondering where to start. Each product has its own access page with its own guides, Claude skills and copy-paste prompts. This page is your map. Follow the 14-day plan below and you'll have your targets, resume, LinkedIn, outreach, interview prep and offer plan ready.

## What's inside

**The five products** (open each one from **Your products** on this page):
- **Career Prompt Vault:** 6 Claude skills and 50 copy-paste prompts for career clarity, target roles and companies, your resume, LinkedIn and more.
- **Salary Number Generator:** a guide and a Claude skill to work out your anchor, target and walk-away numbers before any money conversation.
- **Outreach and Email Templates:** 60+ templates and a Claude skill for cold emails, LinkedIn messages, networking and follow-ups, plus "Land an Interview in 1 Week".
- **Interview Confidence Kit:** guides for your story bank, "Tell me about yourself", likely questions, nerves and follow-up, plus a Claude skill that runs mock interviews with you.
- **Salary Negotiation Scripts and Advice:** 52 scripts, an advice guide, an offer worksheet and a Claude skill that evaluates your offer, drafts your counter and rehearses the call.

**Bonus guides (on this page):**
- **01 Rejection Response Scripts:** 10 scripts for replying to a "no" with grace, asking for feedback and staying on their radar.
- **02 Resignation and Transition Scripts:** 10 scripts for resigning, handling a counter-offer, telling your team and announcing your move.

## The order to use them

A job search has five stages. Here's which product leads each one:

1. **Clarity and targets:** Career Prompt Vault (target roles and companies) and the Salary Number Generator (your three numbers). Know what you want and what you're worth before you write a word.
2. **Resume and LinkedIn:** Career Prompt Vault. Turn your experience into achievements, then make your profile match.
3. **Outreach:** Outreach and Email Templates. Reach real people at your target companies instead of only applying online.
4. **Interviews:** Interview Confidence Kit, plus the bonus Rejection Response Scripts for the ones that don't go your way.
5. **The offer:** Salary Negotiation Scripts and Advice, plus the bonus Resignation and Transition Scripts once you've signed.

You can jump ahead if a stage is already done. Got an interview next week? Start at stage 4.

## Your first 10 minutes

1. Open **Your products** on this page and bookmark each product's access page.
2. Open the Career Prompt Vault and read its Start Here.
3. Write down two or three job titles you're aiming for. Rough is fine.
4. Open the Salary Number Generator and write your current best guess at a target salary. You'll sharpen it on Day 2.

## Using the Claude skills

The skills live on each product's own page. Open **Your products**, go to a product, and open the **Install** tab on that page. Install as many as you like. Claude picks the right skill based on what you ask.

Try these once they're installed:

- "Help me pick two or three target job titles from my resume."
- "Interview me about my achievements and write my resume bullets."
- "Work out my anchor, target and walk-away numbers."
- "Write a cold email to a hiring manager at [Company]."
- "Run a mock interview for this job post, one question at a time."
- "Here's my offer. What should I ask for? Then play the recruiter so I can practise."

## No Claude? Use any AI

1. Open a product from **Your products**, then open its **Copy-paste prompts**.
2. Copy the prompt you need and replace everything in [square brackets] with your real details.
3. Paste it into the AI assistant you already use, answer its questions, and check every fact and number before you use it.

## A 14-day plan

- **Day 1:** Open every product page and install the skills you'll use. Pick two or three target job titles with the Career Prompt Vault.
- **Day 2:** Set your anchor, target and walk-away with the Salary Number Generator.
- **Day 3:** Build a list of target companies with the Career Prompt Vault.
- **Day 4:** Dig up your achievements and draft your resume bullets with the Career Prompt Vault.
- **Day 5:** Finish your resume and summary. Tailor one version to a real job post.
- **Day 6:** Update your LinkedIn headline and About section with the Career Prompt Vault.
- **Day 7:** Take a breather. Review your week and pick 10 people to contact at your target companies.
- **Day 8:** Send your first five messages with the Outreach and Email Templates.
- **Day 9:** Send five more, and set reminders for your follow-ups.
- **Day 10:** Write and practise your "Tell me about yourself" with the Interview Confidence Kit.
- **Day 11:** Build your story bank (aim for six strong stories to start).
- **Day 12:** Run a mock interview with the interview skill. Save the thank-you templates and the bonus Rejection Response Scripts so they're ready.
- **Day 13:** Read "How to Negotiate Your Offer" and practise the salary question out loud.
- **Day 14:** Fill in the offer worksheet for your current job (so you know what you'd give up), then plan next week: more outreach, follow-ups and interview practice.

After Day 14, keep the outreach going every week. When an offer arrives, open the Salary Negotiation kit. When you sign, open the bonus Resignation Scripts.

## Good to know

- **Results vary.** These tools help you prepare well, but nobody can guarantee interviews or offers. Hiring depends on a lot you can't control.
- **Keep your facts true.** Every achievement, number and title should be one you can explain if someone asks. If you estimate a number, say "about" and know how you got it.
- **Salary and negotiation material is general guidance, not legal or financial advice.** For contract clauses or tax questions, talk to an employment lawyer or an accountant.
- **Canada and the US differ** on things like pay transparency, vacation minimums and notice. The guides flag these where they matter.
- **Need help?** Reply to your purchase email and it comes straight to me.
