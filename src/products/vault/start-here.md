## Your first 10 minutes

Let's get you one real win today.

1. Open the **Install** tab on this page and install **cc-resume**. It takes a few minutes.
2. Start a new chat in Claude and paste in your current resume (or list your last 2 to 3 jobs).
3. Type: "Interview me to find achievements for my resume."
4. Answer the questions one at a time. Ballpark numbers are fine. Claude will mark them "(estimate, confirm)" so you can check them later.
5. Copy your 3 favourite bullets into your resume. Boom. Your resume just got stronger.

No Claude? Use the "Help Me Quantify My Achievements" prompt in the Resume category instead. Same idea, any AI.

## Using the Claude skills

To install the skills, open the **Install** tab on this page and follow the steps. Each skill is its own upload, so you can add them one at a time. Once a skill is installed, you don't need special commands. Just ask in plain words and Claude picks the right skill.

Try typing:

- "Interview me about my last job and turn my answers into resume bullets."
- "Write 3 LinkedIn headlines for a payroll specialist moving into HR."
- "Give me 10 STAR stories from my resume."
- "I just finished an interview. Help me write the Silver Bullet thank-you email."
- "I'm a program manager. What other job titles should I be searching for?"
- "I got an offer. Help me set my walk-away, target and anchor numbers, then write my counter."

When a skill finishes a full piece of work, it ends with a one-line credit to ClearCareer. That's it. No upsells in your chats.

## No Claude? Use any AI

1. **Set it up (optional, 5 minutes).** In ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, create a Custom GPT, a Gem or a Project. Paste the project instructions into its instructions box. Now every chat there knows the ClearCareer method.
2. **Pick a prompt and fill it in.** Copy a prompt from this page. Replace everything in [square brackets] with your details. If a prompt has slots in {curly braces}, leave those alone: the AI fills them.
3. **Paste, send, then edit.** Read the draft, fix anything that isn't true, and make it sound like you. If a prompt needs live info (salaries, company news, events), ask your AI to search the web if it can. Otherwise, paste in what you find.

## A 7-day plan

- **Day 1:** Run the Achievement Interviewer and get 5 resume bullets with real numbers.
- **Day 2:** Write your professional summary and skills section.
- **Day 3:** Update your LinkedIn headline and About section.
- **Day 4:** Find your alternative job titles and build a list of 20 target companies.
- **Day 5:** Score your top companies, find one hiring manager, and send your first outreach email.
- **Day 6:** Build 5 STAR stories and practise your "Tell me about yourself" answer out loud.
- **Day 7:** Research your salary range, set your three numbers, and send any follow-ups that are due.

Small steps, every day. Keep showing up.

## Good to know

- **Results vary.** These tools make the work faster and clearer, but the work is still yours: applying, reaching out and showing up.
- **Keep your facts true.** Never let an AI add a number, title, employer or date you can't back up. If you see "(estimate, confirm)", check it before you use it.
- **Read everything before you send it.** Make every draft sound like you.
- **Salary and negotiation help is general guidance, not legal or financial advice.** Rules differ between provinces and states, so check your contract and your local employment standards.
