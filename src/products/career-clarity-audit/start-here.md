# Start here: Career Clarity Audit

Welcome in. This one's free, and it answers two questions that stall a lot of job searches: "Which jobs should I actually be applying for?" and "What should I be asking to get paid?" You give Claude your resume. It gives you back 5 job titles that fit what you've already done, each with a fit score, typical pay, demand and a ready LinkedIn search you can click. Then it shows where your pay sits in the market, with three numbers to bring to any salary conversation: your anchor, your target and your walk-away. It takes about 15 minutes, and you'll use what it gives you for the rest of your search.

## What's inside

- **Guide: How to read your audit.** What your fit scores mean, how to use your titles in your search and on LinkedIn, how to use your three numbers, and what to do this week.
- **Claude skill: cc-clarity-audit.** Reads your resume and builds your full audit: 5 titles, a LinkedIn search for each, and your salary positioning.
- **Its prompt cards:** the same method as copy-paste prompts (find your titles, score a title you're curious about, build LinkedIn searches, position your salary, answer the salary question), so you can use them in any AI tool.

## Your first 10 minutes

1. Install the skill (see below), or open the prompt cards if you're using another AI tool.
2. Start a new chat, attach your resume and type: "Run my Career Clarity Audit for jobs in [City]."
3. Read the "Why it fits" line for each title. If one feels off, tell Claude why and ask it to swap that title.
4. Click the LinkedIn link for your top title and turn on a job alert.

That's it. You now have a clear list of roles to go after, and fresh jobs coming to your inbox.

## Using the Claude skills

To install the skill, open the **Install** tab on this page and follow the steps. You only do it once.

Then talk to Claude normally. Try one of these:

- "Here's my resume. Run my Career Clarity Audit for jobs in Halifax."
- "What job titles should I be searching for with this resume?"
- "How well do I fit Program Manager?"
- "Redo my LinkedIn searches for remote roles in Canada."
- "Here are 4 pay ranges I found. Work out my anchor, target and walk-away."
- "A recruiter asked my salary expectations. What do I say?"

The skill never makes up salary figures. If Claude can search the web, every number comes with a link to where it found it. If it can't, it shows you exactly where to look (job posts with pay ranges, salary sites, and government data like Job Bank in Canada or the Bureau of Labor Statistics in the US) and asks you to paste what you found. Any estimate is labelled "(estimate, confirm)" so you know to check it.

## No Claude? Use any AI

1. Open the prompt cards on this page and start with "Find 5 job titles to target."
2. Copy it, replace everything in [square brackets], and paste your resume where it says to.
3. Paste it into the AI assistant you already use. Check every pay figure against a real source before you rely on it, and then run "Position my salary for my top title."

## A 7-day plan

- **Day 1:** Run your audit and read the guide.
- **Day 2:** Click all 5 LinkedIn links and turn on job alerts for your top 2 or 3 titles.
- **Day 3:** Read 3 job posts for your top title. Highlight what they ask for that your resume doesn't show yet.
- **Day 4:** Update your LinkedIn headline and the top of your resume around your top title.
- **Day 5:** Click every salary source in your audit, and find 3 more job posts with pay ranges.
- **Day 6:** Write your anchor, target and walk-away on a sticky note. Practise your salary answer out loud.
- **Day 7:** Message one person who has one of your 5 titles and ask what the job is really like.

## Good to know

- **It's free.** If you want to go further, the paid Resume and LinkedIn Audit Kit and Land and Level Up are at joinclearcareer.com.
- **Results vary.** Your audit gives you a clear starting point, but no one can promise interviews, offers or a salary.
- **This is general guidance, not legal or financial advice.** Pay data changes and sources disagree, so check your numbers before you use them.
- **Fit scores are a rough guide.** They show how much of a job your resume already proves. They aren't a chance of getting hired.
- **Keep your facts true.** Never change a past job title or stretch your experience to fit a title. Fix how you describe what you really did.
- **Rules vary by place.** Several provinces and US states now require pay ranges in job posts. Rules change, so check the current ones where you live.
- **Need help?** Reply to the email that sent you this audit and it comes straight to me.
