# Start here: Salary Negotiation Scripts and Advice

Welcome in. This kit is for the moment money comes up and your mind goes blank. Maybe a recruiter is about to ask your salary expectations. Maybe there's an offer sitting in your inbox right now. Either way, you'll have the words ready. Inside are 52 word-for-word scripts (email and phone), my advice on when and how to use them, a worksheet for comparing offers, and a Negotiation Coach skill for Claude that evaluates your offer, drafts your counter and plays the recruiter so you can rehearse. Asking feels scary. It gets a lot less scary once you've said the words out loud a few times.

## Your first 10 minutes

1. Write your three numbers on a sticky note: anchor, target and walk-away. No numbers yet? Guide 01 has the short version. The Salary Number Generator has the full method and pairs well with this kit.
2. Read "My Principles" in Guide 01. It takes about five minutes.
3. Find the script for where you are right now: the Quick Picker in Guide 02 or the Jump To table in Guide 05.
4. Fill in the [square brackets] and say it out loud three times.

That's your first win. You now have a plan and the exact words for your next money conversation.

## Using the Claude skills

To install the Negotiation Coach, open the **Install** tab on this page and follow the steps. It takes a few minutes, and you only do it once.

Once it's installed, talk to Claude normally. Try one of these:

- "Here's my offer. Is it good, and what should I ask for?"
- "A recruiter is calling tomorrow. Help me answer the salary question."
- "Write my counter-offer email and a phone script. I want to ask for $[Number]."
- "They say base is fixed. What else can I ask for?"
- "They gave me 24 hours to decide. What do I say?"
- "Play the recruiter and make it hard. Give me feedback after each answer."

The skill never makes up salary data. If it needs a number, it asks you. If it estimates something (like the value of a benefit), it labels it "(estimate, confirm)" so you know to check.

## A 7-day plan

- **Day 1:** Set your three numbers. Write down your must-haves and deal-breakers (Guide 06, Part 1).
- **Day 2:** Read Guide 01 from start to finish.
- **Day 3:** Pick your scripts from Guide 02 and practise the salary question out loud, or run the salary question drill with the skill.
- **Day 4:** Read Guides 03 and 04. Rank your beyond-base asks.
- **Day 5:** Skim Guide 05 so nothing catches you off guard. Mark the two moments you're most worried about.
- **Day 6:** Fill in Guide 06 for your offer. No offer yet? Fill it in for your current job, so you know what you'd be giving up.
- **Day 7:** Draft your counter (Guide 03 or the skill), then rehearse it with the skill at medium and hard difficulty.

Got an offer sooner than 7 days? Do Day 1, Day 6 and Day 7 today, and read Guide 05 while you wait for their reply.

## Good to know

- **This is general guidance, not legal or financial advice.** For contract clauses, a pulled offer or tax on equity, talk to an employment lawyer or an accountant.
- **Results vary.** No script guarantees a higher offer. Every employer, role and market is different.
- **Keep your facts true.** Never bluff about another offer, a deadline or a number. Use real results and real market data you can point to.
- **Canada and the US differ** on pay transparency, pay history questions and vacation minimums. Guide 01 has the short version. Check the rules where you live.
