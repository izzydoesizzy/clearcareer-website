# Start here: Outreach and Email Templates

Welcome! This pack is for anyone who's tired of applying online and hearing nothing back. Here's the belief behind everything inside: don't just apply and wait. Find the people who hire for the role (the hiring manager, the team lead), find their email addresses, send short and specific emails, and then follow up. You'll get the method I teach, 83 ready-to-use templates for every stage of the search, and a Claude skill that personalizes them for you in minutes. If reaching out to strangers makes your stomach flip, you're in the right place. We'll make it feel normal.

## What's inside

- **Land an Interview in 1 Week:** the method, step by step. How to find the hiring manager, work out their email address, write the email, follow up and track it. Includes a day-by-day plan for your first week and 5 templates.
- **Hiring Manager Cold Emails:** 15 short emails to the person who would be your boss, for posted jobs, companies with no posting, career changes, relocations and returning after a break.
- **Follow-Ups:** the cadence (when to follow up, and when to stop) plus 13 templates, from a cold email all the way to a final round.
- **LinkedIn Requests and Messages:** 14 connection notes and messages for hiring managers, alumni, recruiters and peers.
- **Referrals and Informational Interviews:** 13 templates for asking for advice, running the conversation, and turning it into introductions and referrals.
- **Thank-You Emails:** the "Silver Bullet" thank-you that reminds interviewers exactly why you're the answer, plus 9 more thank-you notes.
- **Networking Without the Awkward:** 18 ways to add value, 13 messages to send, icebreakers for events and the follow-ups that come after.
- **The cc-email-writer Claude skill:** give it your resume (or a few wins), the role, the company and the person, and it picks the right template, writes a short email under 120 words with a subject line, and gives you a follow-up plan.

## Your first 10 minutes

1. **Read the six steps** at the top of Land an Interview in 1 Week. (3 minutes)
2. **Pick one company** you'd love to work for. On its LinkedIn page, click People and find the person who would be your boss. (3 minutes)
3. **Copy Template 1 or Template 2** from Hiring Manager Cold Emails, or ask the Claude skill to write it. Fill in the brackets with one real win from your work. (3 minutes)
4. **Save it as a draft.** Tomorrow, work out their email address using Step 3 of the method, and hit send. (1 minute)

That's your first win: a real email to a real decision-maker, ready to go.

## Using the Claude skills

To install the skill, open the **Install** tab on this page and follow the steps there. Once it's installed, start a new chat in Claude and type a request in plain words. Try one of these:

- "Write a cold email to [Name], Director of Finance at [Company], about the Senior Analyst role. Here's my resume."
- "Who should I contact about a UX designer role at [Company], and how do I work out their email address?"
- "I emailed a hiring manager six days ago and heard nothing. Here's what I sent. Write my follow-up."
- "Write a LinkedIn connection request to a fellow [School] grad on the data team at [Company]."
- "I had my final interview today. Here are my notes. Help me write a Silver Bullet thank-you."
- "I have 10 target companies. Help me plan this week."

The skill will ask for anything it's missing. It never invents a shared connection or a fact about the person you're writing to. If it needs a detail only you can find, it leaves a [bracket] and tells you where to look.

## No Claude? Use any AI

1. **Pick a template.** Open the guide that fits your situation and copy the template you want.
2. **Paste it into any AI chat tool** with this prompt:

~~~text
Here is an email template I want to personalize:
[Paste the template]

Here is my background: [Paste your resume, or 2 to 3 wins]
I'm writing to [Person's name and title] at [Company] about [the role or my goal].
Our real connection, if any: [Connection, or "none"]

Fill in the template using only the facts I gave you. Keep it under 120 words. Don't invent any connections, numbers or details about the person. Leave a [bracket] for anything you don't know. Then write a short subject line and two short follow-ups: one for 5 business days later and one for about a week after that.
~~~

3. **Check it and send it yourself.** Read every line, fix anything that isn't true, fill any brackets, and send it from your own email.

## A 7-day plan

- **Day 1:** Pick 10 target companies and the role or team at each. Set up a simple tracker.
- **Day 2:** Find two people per company on LinkedIn: the likely hiring manager and a backup.
- **Day 3:** Work out email addresses for your top 10 people, and note one specific, true detail to open with.
- **Day 4:** Send your first 5 emails. Apply through the careers page first where there's a posting.
- **Day 5:** Send the next 5, then send short LinkedIn connection requests to the same people.
- **Day 6:** Reply to anyone who wrote back, book calls, and prepare a 30-second intro, two wins and three questions.
- **Day 7:** Put your follow-up dates in your calendar, pick your next 10 companies, and rest.

## Good to know

- **Results vary.** Some people get a reply in two days. Some need a few weeks of steady sending. The templates give you a strong start, but nobody can promise a reply or an interview.
- **Keep your facts true.** Never claim a connection you don't have, and never stretch a number. People check, and trust is the whole game.
- **One person at a time.** These are personal, one-to-one emails, not marketing. Follow the spirit of Canada's anti-spam law (CASL) and the US CAN-SPAM Act: use your real name and contact details, never add anyone to a list, and if someone asks you to stop, stop. This is general guidance, not legal advice.
