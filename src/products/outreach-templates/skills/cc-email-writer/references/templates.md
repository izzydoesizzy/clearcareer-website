# Template index

One example of each template type. Match the situation to a type in the table, then adapt the example to the user's facts. Never send an example unchanged, and never fill a bracket with a fact the user didn't give you. The product's guides hold more versions of each type.

| Situation | Template type |
|---|---|
| Posted job, and the user knows who leads the team | Hiring manager: posted job |
| No posting, or recent company news | Hiring manager: no posted job |
| Exploring a field, changing careers, or wants advice | The advice ask |
| Not sure who the right person is | Who's the right person? |
| Large company, or no hiring manager found | Recruiter note |
| A real contact suggested reaching out | Mutual contact (only if true) |
| The user knows someone who knows the person | Introduction request with a blurb |
| A close contact works at the company | Referral ask |
| No reply after 4 to 6 business days | Follow-up 1: the bump |
| Still no reply about a week later | Follow-up 2: the value-add |
| Final touch | The last note |
| Connecting on LinkedIn | LinkedIn connection note |
| They accepted the request | LinkedIn message after connecting |
| After an interview | The Silver Bullet thank-you |
| After a coffee chat or informational interview | Thank-you after a conversation |
| Staying in touch, or reconnecting | Value-first reconnection |

## Hiring manager: posted job

**What it does:** Shows a hiring manager that the user fits the top two priorities in the posting, then asks for a short call.
**When to use it:** There's a posted job and the user knows who leads the team.

### Step 1: Fill in and send

~~~text
Subject: [Job title]: how I'd help with [top priority]

Hi [First name],

I'm reaching out about the [Job title] role. Reading the posting, it sounds like you need someone who can [Priority 1] and [Priority 2].

Here's how I've done both:

1. [Priority 1]: [Win with a number]
2. [Priority 2]: [Win with a number]

I've applied through the careers page. Would you be open to a 20-minute call on [day] to talk about what you need most in this hire?

Thanks,
[Your name]
[Phone] | [LinkedIn URL]
~~~

**Replace:** [Job title], [top priority], [First name], [Priority 1], [Priority 2], [Win with a number], [day], [Your name], [Phone], [LinkedIn URL]

## Hiring manager: no posted job

**What it does:** Uses real company news to open a conversation about a need the user can help with.
**When to use it:** The company had news in the last month or two and there's no matching posting.

### Step 1: Fill in and send

~~~text
Subject: Congrats on [launch, expansion or milestone]

Hi [First name],

I saw [Company] just [launched X / opened in Y / announced Z]. Congrats to you and the team.

In my experience, that kind of growth usually means [a likely need]. At [Current or last company], I [win that addresses that need].

Is that something your team is thinking about? I'd love 15 minutes to compare notes.

Thanks,
[Your name]
[LinkedIn URL]
~~~

**Replace:** [launch, expansion or milestone], [First name], [Company], [launched X / opened in Y / announced Z], [a likely need], [Current or last company], [win that addresses that need], [Your name], [LinkedIn URL]

## The advice ask

**What it does:** Asks for 15 minutes of advice, opening with something specific the person made or said.
**When to use it:** The user is exploring a field or company, changing careers, or there's no posting yet.

### Step 1: Fill in and send

~~~text
Subject: Your [post or talk] on [topic]

Hi [First name],

I came across your [post, talk or podcast episode] on [topic], and [the specific point] stuck with me. It lines up with how I approached [related thing you did].

A bit about me: I'm a [Your title] in [industry]. Most recently, I [win with a number]. I'd like to do more [type of work] at a company like [Company].

Would you be open to a 15-minute call [next Tuesday or Wednesday]? I'd love your advice on how to break into [role or team].

Thanks,
[Your name]
[LinkedIn URL]
~~~

**Replace:** [post or talk], [topic], [First name], [post, talk or podcast episode], [the specific point], [related thing you did], [Your title], [industry], [win with a number], [type of work], [Company], [next Tuesday or Wednesday], [role or team], [Your name], [LinkedIn URL]

## Who's the right person?

**What it does:** Asks someone close to the team to confirm they're the right contact, or point to who is.
**When to use it:** The user can't tell who the hiring manager is.

### Step 1: Fill in and send

~~~text
Subject: Quick question about [team] at [Company]

Hi [First name],

I'm a [Your title], and I'm interested in [team or type of role] at [Company]. I'm trying to reach whoever leads [team or function], and your name came up when I looked at the team.

If that's you, I'd love 15 minutes to learn what the team needs. If it's someone else, would you mind pointing me to them?

Either way, thanks for reading.

[Your name]
[LinkedIn URL]
~~~

**Replace:** [team], [Company], [First name], [Your title], [team or type of role], [team or function], [Your name], [LinkedIn URL]

## Recruiter note

**What it does:** Gives a recruiter the role, the user's title and one win, so they can match fast.
**When to use it:** A large company where recruiters screen first, or no hiring manager can be found.

### Step 1: Fill in and send

~~~text
Subject: [Your title], [number] years in [field]: [Job title]

Hi [First name],

I saw you recruit for [team or function] at [Company], so I wanted to reach out about the [Job title] role [posting ID, if there is one].

I'm a [Your title] with [number] years in [field]. My most relevant win: [win with a number].

I've applied online. Would you be open to a 10-minute call to see if it's a fit? Happy to send my resume.

Thanks,
[Your name]
[Phone] | [LinkedIn URL]
~~~

**Replace:** [Your title], [number], [field], [Job title], [First name], [team or function], [Company], [posting ID, if there is one], [win with a number], [Your name], [Phone], [LinkedIn URL]

## Mutual contact (only if true)

**What it does:** Opens with the name of a real person who suggested the user get in touch.
**When to use it:** Only when a real contact suggested it and agreed to be named. Never use it for a contact the user simply shares on LinkedIn.

### Step 1: Fill in and send

~~~text
Subject: [Mutual contact's name] suggested I reach out

Hi [First name],

[Mutual contact's full name] suggested I get in touch. [He/She/They] mentioned you lead [team] and might be open to a quick chat about [topic or role].

I'm a [Your title] with [number] years in [field]. Most recently, I [win with a number].

Would you have 15 minutes in the next week or two? I'd value your perspective.

Thanks,
[Your name]
[LinkedIn URL]
~~~

**Replace:** [Mutual contact's name], [First name], [Mutual contact's full name], [He/She/They], [team], [topic or role], [Your title], [number], [field], [win with a number], [Your name], [LinkedIn URL]

## Introduction request with a blurb

**What it does:** Asks a contact for an introduction and includes a short blurb they can forward as is.
**When to use it:** The user knows someone who is connected to the person they want to meet.

### Step 1: Fill in and send

~~~text
Subject: Quick favour: an intro to [Target person]?

Hi [First name],

I noticed you're connected to [Target person], who leads [team] at [Company]. I'm interested in [type of role] there. Would you be comfortable introducing us?

Here's a short blurb you can forward, to make it easy:

"[Your name] is a [Your title] with [number] years in [field]. Most recently, [he/she/they] [win with a number]. [He/She/They] would love 15 minutes to learn about your team. Email: [Your email]"

No pressure at all if it doesn't feel right. I appreciate you either way.

[Your name]
~~~

**Replace:** [Target person], [First name], [team], [Company], [type of role], [Your name], [Your title], [number], [field], [he/she/they], [win with a number], [He/She/They], [Your email]

## Referral ask

**What it does:** Asks a close contact to refer the user for a specific posting, with a one-line summary to paste.
**When to use it:** A friend or former colleague works at the company and the user has a real fit for an open role.

### Step 1: Fill in and send

~~~text
Subject: Would you be comfortable referring me?

Hi [First name],

I saw [Company] has a [Job title] opening ([link]), and I think it's a strong fit for my work in [area].

Would you be comfortable referring me? If your company has a referral link, I'll wait and apply through that instead.

To make it easy, here's a one-line summary you can paste: "[Your name] is a [Your title] who [win with a number]."

No pressure. I appreciate you either way.

[Your name]
~~~

**Replace:** [First name], [Company], [Job title], [link], [area], [Your name], [Your title], [win with a number]

## Follow-up 1: the bump

**What it does:** Brings the first email back to the top of their inbox in three lines.
**When to use it:** 4 to 6 business days after the first email, with no reply. Sent as a reply in the same thread.

### Step 1: Reply in the same thread

~~~text
Hi [First name],

Bumping this up in case it got buried. I'd still love 15 minutes to hear about [team or role]. If [day] or [day] works, I'll fit around your schedule.

Thanks,
[Your name]
~~~

**Replace:** [First name], [team or role], [day], [Your name]

## Follow-up 2: the value-add

**What it does:** Gives the person something useful and repeats the ask lightly.
**When to use it:** About a week after follow-up 1, when the user has a relevant article, report or piece of news to share.

### Step 1: Reply in the same thread

~~~text
Hi [First name],

I came across [article, report or news] about [topic] and thought of your team, especially [one line on why it's relevant to them].

[Link]

I'm still keen to chat for 15 minutes about [original topic] if the timing works. Either way, I hope it's useful.

Thanks,
[Your name]
~~~

**Replace:** [First name], [article, report or news], [topic], [one line on why it's relevant to them], [Link], [original topic], [Your name]

## The last note

**What it does:** Closes the loop politely and leaves the door open.
**When to use it:** The third and final touch. After this, the user stops.

### Step 1: Reply in the same thread

~~~text
Hi [First name],

I'll keep this brief. If now isn't the right time, I completely understand, and this is my last note on it.

If things change down the road, I'd love to reconnect. Wishing you and the team a great [month or quarter].

Thanks,
[Your name]
[LinkedIn URL]
~~~

**Replace:** [First name], [month or quarter], [Your name], [LinkedIn URL]

## LinkedIn connection note

**What it does:** Gets the user connected with a short note and no pitch, under 200 characters.
**When to use it:** A day or two after emailing a hiring manager, or with anyone the user wants to connect with on LinkedIn.

### Step 1: Pick one and send

~~~text
After an email: Hi [First name], I applied for the [Job title] role on your team and sent you a short email. Would love to connect here too. Thanks!

After a post: Hi [First name], your post on [topic] stuck with me, especially [one short point]. I work in [field] and would love to follow your thinking. Thanks!

Alumni: Hi [First name], fellow [School] grad here! I'm exploring [field] roles and your path to [Company] caught my eye. Would love to connect.
~~~

**Replace:** [First name], [Job title], [topic], [one short point], [field], [School], [Company]

## LinkedIn message after connecting

**What it does:** Thanks the person for connecting and asks one specific question, plus a short call.
**When to use it:** Within a few days of the person accepting a connection request.

### Step 1: Fill in and send

~~~text
Hi [First name],

Thanks for connecting! I'm reaching out because [specific reason tied to them or their team].

Quick background: I [win with a number] at [Current or last company].

I'm curious about [one specific question about their team or work]. Would you be open to a 15-minute call?

Thanks,
[Your name]
~~~

**Replace:** [First name], [specific reason tied to them or their team], [win with a number], [Current or last company], [one specific question about their team or work], [Your name]

## The Silver Bullet thank-you

**What it does:** Thanks the interviewer, plays back the three things they need, and proves each one with a win.
**When to use it:** Within 24 hours of any interview with a hiring manager, and after every final round. One email per interviewer, with a different memorable moment for each. This is the one email allowed to run up to about 200 words.

### Step 1: Gather the notes

~~~text
Right after the interview, write down:
1. The three things they kept coming back to, in their words. These are the priorities.
2. One moment you enjoyed or learned from.
3. Any concern they raised about your background.
4. Any answer you wish you had given better.
Then match each priority to a win from your resume.
~~~

**Replace:** Nothing. This step is a checklist.

### Step 2: Write and send

~~~text
Subject: Thank you, and a few more thoughts on [Job title]

Hi [First name],

Thank you for taking the time to speak with me today. I really enjoyed [memorable moment].

From our conversation, it sounds like the team needs someone who can [Priority 1], [Priority 2] and [Priority 3]. Here's why I'm confident I can help:

1. [Priority 1]: [Win that proves it, with a number]
2. [Priority 2]: [Win that proves it, with a number]
3. [Priority 3]: [Win that proves it, with a number]

[Optional: You asked about [concern]. I should add that [relevant experience that eases it].]

I'm excited about [Company] because [challenge or opportunity they described], and I'd love to help the team get there.

Thanks again. I look forward to hearing from you.

[Your name]
[Phone] | [LinkedIn URL]
~~~

**Replace:** [Job title], [First name], [memorable moment], [Priority 1], [Priority 2], [Priority 3], [Win that proves it, with a number], [concern], [relevant experience that eases it], [Company], [challenge or opportunity they described], [Your name], [Phone], [LinkedIn URL]

## Thank-you after a conversation

**What it does:** Thanks someone for an informational interview or coffee chat and says what the user will do with their advice.
**When to use it:** Within 24 hours of the conversation.

### Step 1: Fill in and send

~~~text
Subject: Thank you, [First name]

Hi [First name],

Thank you for chatting with me [today or yesterday]. Your advice on [specific topic] was so helpful, especially [one takeaway].

I'm going to [action you'll take based on their advice], and I'll let you know how it goes.

[If they offered an intro: I'd love to take you up on the introduction to [Name]. Just let me know how you'd like to do it.]

If I can ever return the favour, please reach out.

[Your name]
~~~

**Replace:** [First name], [today or yesterday], [specific topic], [one takeaway], [action you'll take based on their advice], [Name], [Your name]

## Value-first reconnection

**What it does:** Reconnects with someone by sharing something useful before any ask.
**When to use it:** Reaching back out to a former colleague or contact the user hasn't spoken to in a while.

### Step 1: Fill in and send

~~~text
Subject: Thought of you: [topic]

Hi [First name],

I came across [article, news or resource] about [topic] and immediately thought of you and your work on [their area]: [Link]

I'm exploring my next role and reconnecting with people I respect in [industry]. Would love to catch up if you have a few minutes sometime.

Either way, hope the link is useful.

[Your name]
~~~

**Replace:** [topic], [First name], [article, news or resource], [their area], [Link], [industry], [Your name]
