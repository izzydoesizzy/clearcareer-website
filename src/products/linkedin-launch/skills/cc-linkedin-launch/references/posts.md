# Post prompts: posts from your own stories

Izzy's 4-post set for job seekers: an Industry Insight, a Career Lesson, a Value-Add Tip and an #OpenToWork announcement, each mined from real things that happened in your work. Use these prompts with the cc-linkedin-launch skill in Claude, or paste them into any AI chat tool. Swap everything in [square brackets] for your own details.

## Write 4 posts from my stories

**What it does:** Finds real stories in your work, then writes 4 ready-to-post LinkedIn posts: an Industry Insight, a Career Lesson, a Value-Add Tip and an #OpenToWork announcement.
**When to use it:** When you want to show up on LinkedIn while you search, without making anything up.

### Step 1: Mine my stories

~~~text
Act as my LinkedIn content coach. I want to write 4 posts, all based on real things that happened in my work. Help me find the stories first.

My resume or LinkedIn profile text:
[Paste your resume or profile]

The job title I'm targeting: [Target job title]

My 3 to 5 career stories or wins (rough notes are fine):
[Your stories or wins]

Ask me ONE question at a time and wait for my answer. Find material for these 4 posts:
1. Industry Insight: a trend or change I've seen in my field, and what it means for people who do this work.
2. Career Lesson: a surprising moment, a mistake or a challenge, what I did about it, and what I learned.
3. Value-Add Tip: something useful I could teach in about 10 minutes, with a real example from my work.
4. #OpenToWork: what I'm looking for next (titles, industries, location or remote) and my 3 to 4 best achievements.

For each story, ask follow-up questions until you know what happened, what I did and what changed, with a number if one exists. Never invent a story or a detail. If I can't think of one for a post type, ask me a better question, or we skip that post.

When you have enough, show me a Story Prompt for each post: 1 to 2 sentences on what story I'll tell and why it works. Then wait for my OK.
~~~

**Replace:** [Paste your resume or profile], [Target job title], [Your stories or wins]

### Step 2: Write the 4 posts

~~~text
Now write the 4 posts from the stories I approved. For each one, give the Story Prompt, then the Sample Post, ready to paste.

POST TYPES (one of each)
1. Industry Insight: a 2-line hook, 3 implications for people in my field, and an engagement question.
2. Career Lesson: the surprising moment or challenge, what I did, what I learned, and a takeaway anyone could use.
3. Value-Add Tip: a 10-minute framework or practical tip from my work, in numbered steps, with a real example.
4. #OpenToWork announcement: professional and specific about what I'm looking for (titles, industries, location or remote), with 3 to 4 of my achievements and one clear way people can help.

FORMAT RULES
- One sentence per line, with a blank line between lines (LinkedIn style).
- 0 to 2 emoji per post.
- No links in the post. If I need to share one, I'll put it in the first comment.
- End each post with an engagement question.
- 3 to 5 relevant hashtags at the very end.
- 1,200 to 1,500 characters per post. Show the character count after each post.

RULES
- Use only my real stories and facts. Never invent a detail, a number, a quote or an outcome. Mark any estimate (estimate, confirm).
- Don't name employers, coworkers or customers unless I said I can share them.
- Write like a person talking: plain words, short sentences. No em dashes or en dashes. No openers like "In today's fast-paced world".
- Before the #OpenToWork post, remind me to check I'm comfortable posting publicly. If I'm employed, remind me that LinkedIn's Open to Work setting can be shown to recruiters only instead.
~~~

**Replace:** Nothing. Paste this after Step 1.

## Turn one story into a post

**What it does:** Turns a single real story into one LinkedIn post, in the post type that fits it best.
**When to use it:** When something happened at work this week that's worth sharing.

### Step 1: Tell the story

~~~text
Here's a real story from my work:
[Your story: what happened, what you did, what changed]

The job title I'm targeting: [Target job title]

Pick the post type that fits best (Industry Insight, Career Lesson or Value-Add Tip) and tell me why in one line. Then write the post:
- One sentence per line, with blank lines between.
- 0 to 2 emoji, no links, an engagement question at the end, and 3 to 5 hashtags.
- 1,200 to 1,500 characters. Show the count.
- Use only what I told you. If something important is missing, ask me before you write. No em dashes or en dashes.
~~~

**Replace:** [Your story: what happened, what you did, what changed], [Target job title]
