# First profile prompts: build your LinkedIn from scratch

Izzy's LinkedIn Profile Build Guide, for people setting up LinkedIn for the first time or starting over: new grads, veterans, career changers and people returning to work. Use these prompts with the cc-linkedin-launch skill in Claude, or paste them into any AI chat tool. Swap everything in [square brackets] for your own details.

## Build my LinkedIn profile from scratch

**What it does:** Writes every section of a first LinkedIn profile, ready to paste: strategy, photo, banner, 3 headlines, About, Experience bullets and skills.
**When to use it:** When you're setting up LinkedIn for the first time, or your profile is so empty it's easier to start over.

### Step 1: Gather the facts

~~~text
Act as my LinkedIn coach. I'm building my LinkedIn profile from scratch, and I want every section written from my real experience.

My resume (or a list of my roles, school, co-op terms, volunteer work and projects):
[Paste your resume]

The job titles I'm targeting: [Target job titles]
Industries I'm aiming for: [Target industries]
Where I want to work: [Location]
What fits me best (new grad, veteran, career changer, returning to work, or other): [Your situation]
The email I want recruiters to use: [Email address]

Before you write anything, ask me ONE question at a time (up to 8 questions) to fill the gaps:
- My 3 to 5 best wins, with numbers if they exist.
- Which 3 roles (jobs, co-op terms, military roles, volunteer roles or major projects) matter most for the jobs I want.
- If I'm a veteran: what my military roles involved, in plain civilian words, and anything I can't share publicly.
- If I'm changing careers: which parts of my past work look most like the new job.
- If I'm a new grad: co-op terms, internships, part-time work, volunteer roles, school projects and awards.

Never invent a win or a number. If I don't know a number, help me work out an honest estimate and mark it (estimate, confirm). When you have enough, tell me, and wait for my OK.
~~~

**Replace:** [Paste your resume], [Target job titles], [Target industries], [Location], [Your situation], [Email address]

### Step 2: Write the profile

~~~text
Now write my complete LinkedIn Profile Build Guide. Write the guidance to me in second person ("you") and the About section in first person ("I"). Use only facts I've given you.

Use exactly these sections, in this order, with a line (---) between sections:

1. OVERALL STRATEGY
One bolded sentence: the single most important positioning decision for this profile.
Then 2 to 3 plain sentences: who this profile should attract, what story it tells, and what someone should understand about me in the first 5 seconds.

2. PROFILE PHOTO
3 to 5 specific action items, each with a bold label and one line of guidance.

3. BANNER
2 to 3 specific action items. For example, a simple text banner with my name and target role.

4. HEADLINE
3 options, each up to 220 characters, with the character count after each:
Safe/Direct: clear, keyword-rich and professional.
Result-Focused: leads with a key result.
Distinctive/Niche: a specific angle from my most unusual experience.
Then: Recommended: Version [X], because [one-line reason].

5. ABOUT ME
A complete, first-person About section, ready to paste, with no placeholders. 3 to 5 short paragraphs: an opening hook, a sentence on what I do and for whom, proof points with real numbers and roles, what I'm looking for now, and a closing call to action with my email. Up to 2,600 characters.

6. WORK EXPERIENCE
For my 3 most recent relevant roles, 4 to 5 bullets each, in this format:
[Job title] | [Company] | [Dates]
- Strong action verb + what I did + the result (only real numbers)

7. SKILLS TO ADD
12 to 15 skills as keywords, grouped into 2 to 4 categories that fit my target roles (for example Operations, Tools and Systems, People). Use the words employers use in job posts for these roles.

RULES
- Every headline, bullet and skill must come from my real experience. No generic filler.
- Use only numbers I gave you or confirmed. Mark estimates (estimate, confirm).
- Start bullets with strong verbs, like Managed, Delivered, Reduced, Built, Trained or Coordinated.
- If I'm a veteran: translate ranks, unit names and acronyms into civilian terms (team size, budget, equipment, outcomes). Leave out anything I said I can't share.
- If I'm changing careers: lead with the role I'm going for, and back it with results from my past work that fit it.
- If I'm a new grad: count co-op terms, internships, part-time work, volunteer work and school projects as real experience.
- Warm and direct, like a coach working beside me. No em dashes or en dashes. No preamble before the Overall Strategy.
~~~

**Replace:** Nothing. Paste this after Step 1.
