# Start here: The Interview Confidence Kit

Welcome in. If you've ever walked out of an interview thinking "Why did I say THAT?", this kit is for you. I built it from the way I prep the people I coach: we find your real stories, we sharpen how you open, we practise until the hard questions feel boring, and we follow up in a way the hiring manager remembers. You'll find eight short guides you can read and fill in, plus an Interview Coach skill for Claude that interviews you, runs mock interviews and writes your thank-you emails with you. Start small. One good story today beats a perfect plan next week.

## Your first 10 minutes

1. Open **Guide 03** and pick the formula that fits you (most people start with Present, Past, Future).
2. Fill in the brackets with your real details. Don't polish yet. Just get it down.
3. Read it out loud once and time it. Aim for 60 to 90 seconds.
4. Open **Guide 01**, read Prompt 5 ("Times you hit a number"), and write down one story idea.

That's it. You now have an opening answer and your first story. Everything else builds on those two.

Once it's installed, just talk to Claude normally. Try one of these:

## A 7-day plan

- **Day 1:** Write your "Tell me about yourself" (Guide 03) and say it out loud three times.
- **Day 2:** Mine your stories with the 8 prompts in Guide 01, or let the skill interview you.
- **Day 3:** Write up your top 6 stories and dig for a number in each one.
- **Day 4:** Map your stories to common questions (Guide 06, Part 2) and fill any gaps.
- **Day 5:** Read Guide 04 and Guide 07. Practise your 5 hardest questions out loud.
- **Day 6:** Run a full mock interview and fix your weakest two answers.
- **Day 7:** Fill in a company prep sheet (Guide 06), pick your questions to ask, and save the thank-you templates from Guide 08 so they're ready.

Got an interview sooner than 7 days? Do Days 1, 3 and 6, then work through the checklist in Guide 06.

## Good to know

- **Results vary.** This kit will help you prepare well and show up calmer, but nobody can guarantee an offer. Hiring depends on a lot you can't control.
- **Keep your facts true.** Every story, number and title you use should be one you can explain if they ask a follow-up. If you estimate a number, say "about" and know how you got it.
- **Salary scripts** in Guide 07 are general guidance, not legal or financial advice.
- **Nerves are normal.** Guide 02 has tips that help most people, but they aren't medical advice. If anxiety is affecting your daily life, talk to your doctor.
- **Need help?** Reply to your purchase email and it comes straight to me.

You've got the tools. Now go practise out loud. That's where the confidence comes from.
