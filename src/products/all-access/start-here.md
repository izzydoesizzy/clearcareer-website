# Start here: The All-Access Pass

Hey, I'm Izzy. This is everything I use with my coaching clients, in one place: 9 products and 17 Claude skills that cover the whole search, from figuring out what to aim for to signing the offer. You don't need all of it this week. Use the order below and pick up each tool when you reach that stage.

## What's inside

Open **Your products** on this page. Each product has its own page with its Claude skills, copy-paste prompts, guides and a Start Here of its own.

- **Career Prompt Vault:** 6 Claude skills (resume, LinkedIn, interviews, outreach, research, offers), including the Achievement Interviewer, plus 50 copy-paste prompts.
- **Resume and LinkedIn Audit Kit:** a snapshot, a full audit or a Rewrite Guide for your resume and your LinkedIn.
- **LinkedIn Launch Pack:** my headline and 4-part About formats, messages for 5 situations, and posts from your stories.
- **Research and Outreach Kit:** score this week's jobs out of 12, rank your target companies, and email the people who hire.
- **Outreach and Email Templates:** 80+ templates for cold emails, follow-ups, LinkedIn, referrals and thank-yous.
- **Interview Confidence Kit:** STAR stories, mock interviews with a Claude coach, answer frameworks and thank-you emails.
- **Interview Prep Dossier:** a full prep pack for one specific job. Run it every time an interview is booked.
- **Salary Number Generator:** your anchor, target and walk-away numbers.
- **Salary Negotiation Scripts and Advice:** the words for every stage of the offer, and a coach that rehearses the call with you.
- **Complete Job Search Toolkit page:** bonus rejection response and resignation scripts.

## Your first 10 minutes

1. Open the **Career Prompt Vault** and install `cc-resume` (the **Install** tab walks you through it).
2. Ask Claude: "Use cc-resume to interview me about my last role." Answer a few questions.
3. Save the bullets it gives you. That's your first win, and it feeds everything else.

## Using the Claude skills

Every product page has an **Install** tab. Each skill is a small zip of text files that you upload once in Claude (Settings, then Capabilities, then Skills). You don't need all 17. Install the ones for the stage you're in, and add more as you go.

Some good first requests:
- "Use cc-resume-review to run a Full audit on my resume for a senior analyst role."
- "Use cc-linkedin-launch to write my headline and About section."
- "Use cc-job-scan to find and score this week's project manager jobs in Toronto."
- "Use cc-interview-dossier to build my prep pack for this job." (then paste the job post)

## No Claude? Use any AI

1. Open any product's **Copy-paste prompts** section.
2. Attach your resume in ChatGPT, Gemini or any AI chat.
3. Paste a prompt, fill in the [brackets], and send.

## A 14-day plan

- **Day 1:** Achievement Interviewer (Prompt Vault). Get 10 strong bullets.
- **Day 2:** Resume audit (Audit Kit), Full depth. Fix the top three things.
- **Day 3:** LinkedIn audit from screenshots, then your headline and About (LinkedIn Launch Pack).
- **Day 4:** Salary Number Generator. Know your three numbers.
- **Day 5:** Job scan (Research and Outreach Kit). Keep the jobs that score 9 or more.
- **Day 6:** Company scorecards. Pick your top 10 companies.
- **Day 7:** Rest, or catch up.
- **Day 8:** Outreach campaign for your top 5 companies. Send 2 emails.
- **Day 9:** LinkedIn messages (Launch Pack) to 5 people. Send 3 more emails.
- **Day 10:** Build 5 STAR stories (Interview Confidence Kit).
- **Day 11:** Run a mock interview. Follow up on day-8 emails.
- **Day 12:** Your first post from your own story (Launch Pack).
- **Day 13:** When an interview is booked, run the Interview Prep Dossier.
- **Day 14:** Review: what got replies, what didn't, and plan next week.

When an offer arrives, open the Salary Negotiation Scripts before you reply.

## Good to know

- Results vary. These tools make the work faster and clearer, but they can't promise a job.
- Keep your facts true. The skills never invent numbers, and you should never let any AI do it for you.
- Salary and negotiation material is general guidance, not legal or financial advice.
