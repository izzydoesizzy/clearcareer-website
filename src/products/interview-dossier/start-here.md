# Start here: Interview Prep Dossier

Welcome in. When someone I coach lands an interview for a job they really want, I build them a prep pack for that one job: the company, the people they're meeting, the doubts the hiring manager might have, their answers, their stories and their questions. It's my flagship deliverable, and it usually runs 15 to 20 pages. This product turns it into a Claude skill you run yourself, for any job, before every interview. You bring the job post and your resume. Claude does the research, links its sources and builds your dossier section by section, using only your real experience. You do the part that matters most: you check it, and you practise it out loud.

## What's inside

**Guides (read on this page):**

- **02 Interview Day Tips:** before, during and after the interview, plus video calls, phone and in-person tips, body language and a 10-minute checklist.

It can also build a 24-hour version, quiz you one question at a time, and update your dossier after each round.

## Your first 10 minutes

1. Install the skill (see the next section). You only do it once.
2. Copy the full text of the job post you're interviewing for, and have your resume ready.
3. In Claude, type "Build my interview dossier." Paste the job post and your resume, and add the interview stage, the date and who you're meeting (if you know).
4. When Part 1 arrives, click two or three of the source links and check the names of the people you're meeting.
5. Reply "next" and keep going.

That's your first win: the research half of your dossier, checked by you, before you've written a single answer.

## Using the Claude skills

To install the Interview Prep Dossier skill, open the **Install** tab on this page and follow the steps. It takes a few minutes.

Once it's installed, talk to Claude normally. Try one of these:

- "Build my interview dossier. Here's the job post and my resume. Hiring manager interview on Thursday at 2 p.m."
- "My interview is tomorrow morning. Give me the 24-hour version."
- "Just do the employer objections for this job."
- "I'm meeting [Name], the VP of Finance, in the final round. Update who I'm meeting and my questions to ask."
- "Quiz me from my dossier. One question at a time. Mix in the objections."
- "Round 1 is done. Here are my notes. Update my dossier for round 2."

If Claude can search the web where you use it, it researches the company and links every source. If it can't, it tells you exactly what to look up and paste in. Either way, it never makes up people, numbers or reviews, and it asks before it estimates anything about you. When your dossier is done, it offers to turn it into a downloadable file if it can create files where you're working.

## No Claude? Use any AI

1. Open the skill's prompt cards on this page. Start with "Read the job post", then run the sections in order.
2. Copy a prompt, replace everything in [square brackets] with your own details, and paste it into the AI tool you already use.
3. Paste each finished section into one document, then check every fact, name and link before you rely on it.

## A 7-day plan

Interview a week away? Here's the pace I'd set.

- **Day 1:** Build Part 1 (the research). Check the links, the dates and the people.
- **Day 2:** Build Parts 2 to 4. Fill in every "Details to confirm" item.
- **Day 3:** Say your "Tell me about yourself" out loud 5 times. Time it and trim it.
- **Day 4:** Practise your 3 hardest objections and your top 4 stories, out loud.
- **Day 5:** Run a quiz with the skill. Redo your 2 weakest answers.
- **Day 6:** Pick your questions to ask, set your salary numbers, and work through the "Before the interview" tips in Guide 02.
- **Day 7 (the day before):** One short quiz, reread Sections 4 and 10, then rest.

Interview sooner? Guide 01 has a 3-day plan, and the skill can build a 24-hour version.

## Good to know

- **Results vary.** A dossier helps you prepare well and show up calmer. Nobody can guarantee an offer.
- **Keep your facts true.** Every story, number and title in your dossier should be one you can explain if they ask a follow-up. If something is marked "(estimate, confirm)", confirm it.
- **Check the research.** AI research can be wrong or out of date. Click the links and check names before you rely on them.
- **Salary numbers:** This is general guidance, not legal or financial advice. Pay rules differ between provinces and states, so check where you live.
- **Need help?** Reply to your purchase email and it comes straight to me.

You bring the job post. Your dossier brings the plan. The reps out loud are where the confidence comes from.
