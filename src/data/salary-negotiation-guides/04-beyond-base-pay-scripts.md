# Beyond Base Pay Scripts

**Scripts 22 to 32: what to ask for when base salary is capped, or when something else matters more.**

> **This is general guidance, not legal or financial advice. Results vary.** Policies on bonuses, equity, vacation and benefits differ from company to company. Get any agreed change in writing.

**Time:** 15 minutes to read.
**What you'll need:** The written offer, your ranked list of what matters most to you, and your current package (so you know what you'd be giving up).

---

Sometimes the base really is capped. Salary bands are real, and a recruiter may not be able to move them.

That doesn't mean the conversation is over. It means you switch to the other parts of the package. And sometimes those parts matter more to you than another few thousand in base anyway.

## First, Rank Your List

Before you ask for anything, rank what matters to you. Put a 1, 2 and 3 next to your top three:

| Item | My rank |
|---|---|
| Signing bonus | |
| Start date | |
| Title | |
| Vacation | |
| Remote or hybrid | |
| Learning budget | |
| Equity | |
| Early salary review | |
| Annual bonus | |
| Relocation | |
| Benefits from day one | |

Ask for your top one or two. Hold the rest in reserve. A list of eleven asks reads like a demand. Two clear asks read like someone who knows what they want.

## The Scripts

### Script 22: Signing bonus to close the gap

~~~text
I understand base salary has limits at this level. Would a signing bonus of $[Amount] be possible to close the gap for the first year? If we can make that work, I'm ready to accept.
~~~

**How much?** A simple, logical number: the difference between their base and your target. That's the gap you're closing for year one.

**Check the clawback.** Many signing bonuses have to be paid back if you leave within a set time. Ask how long, and whether it's prorated.

### Script 23: Start date

~~~text
I'd love to give my current team a proper handoff and take a short break before I start. Would a start date of [Date] work for you?
~~~

**Why it's a good ask:** It costs them little, which makes it an easier yes than money. A week or two between jobs is worth more than it sounds.

### Script 24: Title

~~~text
Based on the scope we talked about, including [specific responsibility, like leading the team or owning the budget], would you be open to the title of [Senior or Lead title]? It reflects the level of the work, and it matters to me for how the role is seen inside and outside the company.
~~~

**Good to know:** Some companies tie titles to pay bands. If the title moves, the band might too. Worth asking.

### Script 25: Vacation

~~~text
Time off matters to me for doing my best work over the long run. I currently have [Number] weeks of vacation. Could we match that, or add one more week to the standard?
~~~

**Your strongest case** is matching what you already have. Nobody wants a new job to feel like a step back.

### Script 26: Remote or hybrid

~~~text
I've worked [remotely or hybrid] for [Number] years and I do my best work in that setup. Would you be open to [your preferred arrangement, like fully remote or one day a week in the office]? If not, could we agree on [compromise] and put it in the offer letter?
~~~

**Get it in writing.** "We're pretty flexible" in an interview is not the same as a written arrangement. Policies change. Offer letters are harder to change.

### Script 27: Learning budget

~~~text
Growing in this role matters to me. Is there a professional development budget? I'm specifically interested in [certification, course or conference], which ties directly to [part of the role]. Would $[Amount] a year be possible?
~~~

**Why it works:** You've named the exact thing and linked it to their work. That's much easier to approve than "Do you have a training budget?"

### Script 28: Equity

~~~text
Can we talk about the equity part of the offer? I'd like to understand the grant: the type, the number of [shares, options or units], the vesting schedule, and the latest valuation or share price. Given the gap on base, is there flexibility to increase the grant to [Number]?
~~~

**Good to know:**
- Equity in a private company is hard to value. Treat its value as uncertain until there's a way to sell it.
- Tax on equity works differently in Canada and the US. Talk to an accountant before you count on it.

### Script 29: An early salary review, in writing

~~~text
If base is firm at $[Their number], could we agree on a formal review at six months, with a path to $[Target] if I hit [specific goals]? I'd be comfortable starting at the current number if we put that in the offer letter.
~~~

**The key words are "in the offer letter."** A friendly "we'll revisit it" is easy to forget. A written date, goals and number are not.

### Script 30: Annual bonus

~~~text
Could we look at the bonus? By leaving now, I'll give up [my bonus for this year / a bonus I'm on track to earn]. Would a guaranteed bonus of $[Amount] for the first year, or a target of [Percent] percent, be possible?
~~~

**Use it when:** You're walking away from money at your current job. It's a fair, concrete reason.

### Script 31: Relocation

~~~text
Since the role means moving to [City], could we talk about relocation support? I'm estimating about $[Amount] for moving costs, plus [Number] weeks of temporary housing.
~~~

**Check for a repayment clause.** Like signing bonuses, relocation money sometimes has to be paid back if you leave early.

### Script 32: Benefits from day one

~~~text
I noticed benefits start after [waiting period]. Since my current coverage ends when I leave, would it be possible for coverage to start on my first day?
~~~

**Matters most if** you or your family would have a gap in health or dental coverage between jobs.

## Putting Two Asks Together

If base is firm and you have two asks, put them in one message:

~~~text
I understand the base is set at this level, and I appreciate you checking. Two things would make this an easy yes for me: a signing bonus of $[Amount], and a start date of [Date]. If we can do those, I'm ready to accept.
~~~

(That's a combination of Scripts 22 and 23, not a new script.)

## Cheat Sheet

| If this is true | Ask for |
|---|---|
| Base is capped but you're short this year | Signing bonus (Script 22) |
| You need a breather between jobs | Start date (Script 23) |
| The scope is bigger than the title | Title (Script 24) |
| You'd lose vacation by switching | Vacation (Script 25) |
| Flexibility is a must-have | Remote or hybrid in writing (Script 26) |
| You want to keep growing | Learning budget (Script 27) |
| It's a startup or public company with stock | Equity (Script 28) |
| They say "we'll revisit it later" | Early review in writing (Script 29) |
| You're giving up a bonus | First-year bonus (Script 30) |
| You have to move | Relocation (Script 31) |
| You'd have a gap in coverage | Benefits from day one (Script 32) |

*This is general guidance, not legal or financial advice. Results vary.*
